## BC8 v2
[SIMULATED, DGX host, nr_td_sim @490d1536fe] slot-model baselines, `--acq 500 --seed 1` (4 RNTIs per acquisition = 2000 RNTIs per row), rx = 4 and 1, 8 parallel processes.
Common flags: `--slot-model 1 --grant-prob 0.5` (default; i.i.d. per eligible DL slot unless `--adjacency` is given), `--dci-false 0`, cap_s 3600, levers C/P/E off, field book 0.
Arm name key: o0/o1 = `--oracle`; L1/L0 = `--k0-oracle-legacy` (L1 = the K39 bug in the slot model, L0 = K39 fix: the oracle never prunes on k0);
rho = `--persist`; m = `--dci-miss`; adj0 / adj1.0 = `--adjacency` (0 = never two consecutive grants, 1 = every eligible slot); tdd = `--tdd DDDSU`.
Design: the product oracle {0,1} x legacy {1,0} (oracle 1 only; legacy is meaningless with the oracle off) x rho {0.5, 0.9} x dci-miss {0.01, 0.1}
is small enough (~10 min wall for all 38 runs) to run in full, plus adjacency {0, 1.0} and TDD DDDSU stress arms at rho 0.9 / miss 0.01. The default
adjacency is "as produced by the model" (adj_frac column, i.i.d. grant-prob 0.5 gives ~0.75 of grants with a same-RNTI PDSCH in a neighbouring slot).
Columns: wrong / undec = RNTIs; truth_k0=N n/wrong/undec = subset whose TRUE k0 is N (k0 = 1 share = 1072/2000 = 53.6 %, drawn uniformly from the catalogue as in v1);
mean_s = mean decided seconds (grants / 200); k0_trap_passes = PHYSICAL shifted-slot trap passes; adj_frac = grants with a same-RNTI PDSCH in an adjacent slot;
f_S = certified_grants / processed grants (certified for the TRUTH's k0 leader, observed DCIs only); cert_sib = (grant, sibling leader) pairs certified;
cert_wrong = such pairs certified although the real occupant of the shifted slot is compatible (real DCI missed AND stood in for by a spurious DCI, OR, when the oracle
destructively pruned the true k0 from the catalogue (L1 arms), the vacuous certification over a truncated sibling set, i.e. assumption A2 violated; dci_false is 0 here).
