# R6 — continuous low-duty discovery

Host: DGX aarch64 (`spark-74c3`). Branch: `rr/reconfig-robustness`.

## Changes and decisions

- [OFFLINE VERIFIED] `nr_pdcch_blind_monitor.{c,h}` adds a pure duty scheduler: the first bank entry leaves full discovery intact; thereafter `ISAC_RECONF=1` samples Technique A once per 20 eligible receive-thread calls by default. `ISAC_RECONF_DISCOVERY_DUTY` sets the divisor. The existing scan-consumer discovery pass verifies and banks any new extent.
- [OFFLINE VERIFIED] Technique A correlation hits in a six-RB window outside every active banked extent now feed R5's `traffic_elsewhere` input. Known CORESET#0 windows are excluded from this occupancy signal; R5's unique CORESET#0 C-RNTI accepts remain a separate source. The hit is handed from the receive thread to the scan consumer through an atomic flag. This also works with one bank and no CORESET#0 or SIB1.
- [IMPLEMENTED, NOT VALIDATED on live reconfiguration] `nr_pdcch_blind_monitor_rt.c` has only the receive-thread duty gate and the bank-tick handoff. The flag-off path keeps its previous paused and unpaused decisions. The GPU nID sweep remains the existing out-of-process snapshot tool; its use and timing are deferred below.
- [OFFLINE VERIFIED] `tests/nr_pdcch_blind_monitor_test.cc` checks the exact 1/20 schedule and flag-off behavior. `tests/nr_pdcch_coreset_bank_test.cc` checks that outside occupancy makes a single SIB1-absent bank STALE, while an idle single bank stays VERIFIED.
