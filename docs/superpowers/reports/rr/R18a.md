# R18a — CSI-RS instrumentation and map-change epoch signal

Host: DGX Spark (`spark-74c3`, aarch64); branch `rr/reconfig-robustness`.

## Changes and decisions

- [IMPLEMENTED, NOT VALIDATED on a live bed] `nr_csirs_blind_rt.{c,h}` counts scored candidates, NZP/ZP confirmations, revocations, and ZP exports. With `ISAC_PDCCH_TIMING=1`, it measures blind-search, ID-sweep, and confirmation microseconds and reports cumulative values in `ISAC_METRICS` plus a periodic `CSIRS_TIMING` per-slot average. `csi_rx.c` times configured CSI-RS CFR extraction and sensing submission. Search time includes the nested ID and confirmation work; the component timings are diagnostic breakdowns, not additive totals.
- [IMPLEMENTED, NOT VALIDATED on a live bed] `nr_csirs_observer.{c,h}` keeps a first-seen slot for each resource and exports a bounded per-resource confirmation latency list in `ISAC_METRICS` (up to 16 resources), with cumulative counters and totals. The JSON field `csirs_time_to_confirm_resources` records `zp`, row, ports, density, period, offsets, frequency domain, and elapsed slots. `nr_passive_metrics.{c,h}` and `_json.c` carry the snapshot.
- [IMPLEMENTED, NOT VALIDATED on a live bed] A different confirmed resource or four scored missed occasions of a confirmed NZP resource signal `nr_cfg_epoch_note_csirs_map_change()`; the epoch owner emits `SOFT / CSIRS_MAP_CHANGE` only with `ISAC_RECONF=1`. One missed confirmation does not bump. Decoded-grant ZP revocation is counted but does not alone declare a cell-map change: it can also withdraw a false ZP hypothesis. Default `ISAC_RECONF` unset/0 does not schedule NZP maintenance scoring or bump the epoch.
- [IMPLEMENTED, NOT VALIDATED on a live bed] `tests/passive_rx/dgx/csirs_arm.sh` wraps `rfsim_arm.sh` with `MODE=off|blind|idsweep`, `RXANT=1|4`, and timing enabled. The wrapper was created but not run.
- [OFFLINE VERIFIED] `tests/nr_csirs_observer_test.cc` covers counter/JSON plumbing and the three requested map-change cases. `CMakeLists.txt` links the observer into the receiver and test targets.
