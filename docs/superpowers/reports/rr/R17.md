## Changes

- [IMPLEMENTED, NOT VALIDATED on live traffic] Added the per-UE context module and schema comment in openair1/PHY/NR_UE_TRANSPORT/nr_passive_ue_ctx.{c,h}. A bounded MPSC sequence ring copies observation, parameter, anchor, SIB1 and epoch events without a producer mutex or I/O. A single worker owns contexts, life-cycle aging, grouping and JSONL append. Full rings and saturated context capacity drop and count events. Closing drains accepted events and writes a final snapshot of every retained context.
- [IMPLEMENTED, NOT VALIDATED on live traffic] ISAC_RECONF=1 plus ISAC_UECTX_PATH opens the writer; without the path or with reconfiguration disabled, hooks remain inactive. The observation producers feed UE statistics independently of ISAC_OBS_PATH. The PDCCH monitor feeds RNTI/CORESET/length/BWP evidence, bank removal marks a UE's CORESET suspect, the RAR chain feeds TC-RNTI anchors, the Technique D reporter feeds winner/state/MCS/DM-RS facts, and the epoch listener turns known values into HINT on SOFT/HARD_REVERIFY or closes old identities on HARD_RESET. Shutdown logs UECTX_STATS events/written/dropped.
- [IMPLEMENTED, NOT VALIDATED on live traffic] SIB1 semantic hash, initial DL BWP and common TDRA list hash are recorded when decoded on either SA or NSA, and remain null without a decoded SIB1. The offline tool tests/passive_rx/uectx/uectx_report.py prints per-UE timelines and change/reconfiguration tables, filters by RNTI and exports CSV.
- [CODE-READ] RNTI class/anchor and cell-common SIB1 changes are logged without inferring a dedicated UE reconfiguration. Per-grant MCS and PRB variation updates statistics only. Trusted changes within two seconds group into one ue_reconfig; one grouped event per UE is sent to the central DEDICATED_CHANGE_SUSPECTED source. Existing R3 SUSPECT notifications remain active only when the UeContext writer is off, preserving the default path.
