# R9 — queued jobs carry the configuration epoch

Host: DGX Spark (`spark-74c3`, aarch64). Branch: `rr/reconfig-robustness`.

## Changes and decisions

- [IMPLEMENTED, NOT VALIDATED on live traffic] `nr_pdcch_passive_queue.{c,h}`, `nr_pdsch_passive_queue.{c,h}`, and `nr_pusch_passive_queue.{c,h}` capture the central `nr_cfg_epoch_current()` at admission with `ISAC_RECONF=1`. Consumers discard older jobs before scan, decode, probes, or feedback. PDSCH filters each member of a slot group before shared work and checks again before each decode and feedback. Its GPU worker request carries the epoch and refuses stale batches; eligible GPU items are checked individually before packing. PUSCH checks again before its UL feedback. Flag-off jobs carry zero and keep the prior path.
- [OFFLINE VERIFIED] `nr_passive_job_epoch.h` supplies the small admission/rejection predicate with an injectable epoch reader. `tests/nr_passive_job_epoch_test.cc` uses a fake reader in `OldEpochJobDropped`, covering same epoch, bumped epoch, and flag-off behavior. `CMakeLists.txt` registers the test.
- [OFFLINE VERIFIED] `nr_passive_metrics.{c,h}`, `nr_passive_metrics_json.c`, and `tests/nr_passive_metrics_test.cc` export and assert `scanq_drop_epoch`, `pdschq_drop_epoch`, and `puschq_drop_epoch` in `ISAC_METRICS`. Each queue maintains its own counter.
- [IMPLEMENTED, NOT VALIDATED on live traffic] An epoch bump during a scan or PHY decode can race with effects inside those routines. This task rejects old jobs at dequeue and checks PDSCH/PUSCH again before queue-owned feedback; it does not make the entire in-flight PHY operation transactional. R10's consumer epoch handling must cover learned state reached inside those routines.
