## Method (ssb_search2.py)
- Four 1 s captures `ota_runs/survey_1s_1011/c3330,c3430,c3550,c3610` (4 ch x 1 s). Calibration on `raw_pci415_12s_b` and
  `raw_pci923_tim_4s_1010_1853` (first 1 s of each). The old 0.3 s survey captures are no longer on disk, so "old method" is emulated on the same data.
- 20 ms chunks, FFT, each GSCN band (N=0.. 3000+1.44N MHz, PSS centre = GSCN - 30 kHz) cut out in frequency domain (= shift + /16), PSS
  correlation by FFT, |cc|^2 summed over the 4 channels (non-coherent) and over all 50 chunks folded modulo k x 20 ms (k=1,2,4,8, SSB period 20..160 ms).
  Metric = folded peak / folded median [dB], reported as "null+X" = excess over the median of that metric over all GSCNs of the scan (the noise-only level).
- SSS -> PCI at the folded burst times (4 ch x all occurrences, equalised by the PSS channel), ratio = best/second-best SSS amplitude (>2 reliable).
- PBCH DMRS (3 symbols, 144 QPSK pilots, c_init from PCI and i_ssb 0..7, delay search +-1 sample) -> i_ssb, ratio best/second (>2 confirms the PCI).
- 15 kHz: NR PSS and LTE PSS (ZC 25/29/34) on a 100 kHz raster over the first 200 ms (LTE folded at 5 ms). LTE SSS not implemented.
- `cp`: CP autocorrelation of a band-limited 30.72 MS/s signal, folded at the 0.5 ms slot, 100 ms segments, hypotheses 15 kHz (N=2048, CP=144) and
  30 kHz (N=1024, CP=72). `tdd`: band power folded at 10 ms (0.25 ms bins). `psd`: 2 MHz bins, dB over the 5th percentile.
- Logs: all runs used `heavy.sh`; full command lines are in the usage block of the script.
