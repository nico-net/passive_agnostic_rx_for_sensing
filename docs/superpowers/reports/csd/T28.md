## Why evidence is lost

[CODE-READ] `nr_csirs_disc.c:284` configures a maximum of four lattice slots per
hypothesis. The original CPU/GPU sweeps choose the newest matching slots from
the current ring, reduce all IDs, and discard the vector. Reject caching waits
`max(2000,3*period)` **accepted ring writes**, not elapsed air slots. An empty
lattice is also rejected/cached. U2's persistent folded energy lattice is not
U4's sequence evidence: thousands of footprints/hypotheses do not mean thousands
of independent sequence observations for any one resource.

[ANALYTICAL, configured mu=1 feed] The 5120-air-slot acquisition interval lasts
2.56 s and offers 2560 slots in alternating 320-slot windows. A P160 phase has
up to 16 opportunities before drops/TDD selection. Steady acquisition offers
640 consecutive slots every 4480 air slots (2.24 s): four opportunities per
P160 phase per window. A four-slot sweep discards older opportunities even if
the footprint recurs. At the ideal steady feed rate, the 2000-write retry delay
is about 7 seconds; discrete windows and drops can extend it. At t26o9's
observed total 29853 FEP slots over the operator's 180-second run, the coarse
run-average equivalent is about **12 seconds per 2000 writes**. This is an
estimate, not a measured per-hypothesis retry interval.

[PARTIAL] None of the six logs has `CSIRS_DISC DIAG` records, nor the full ID
vectors or swept absolute-slot lists. Actual n_slots, repeat overlap, individual
hypothesis revisit times, and rejected z distributions cannot be reconstructed.
They are explicitly unknown in the analyzer. The code proves the per-sweep cap
and absence of sequence accumulation; it does not prove the bad run's exact
per-hypothesis sample counts. With T28 and `ISAC_CSIRS_DISC_DIAG=1`, `EVIDENCE`
logs report total/fresh slots and epoch for each row-1 score vector.

[CODE-READ] Admission is not confirmation. The existing real-time scorer keeps
z>=4 for a single-phase resource; a row-1 resource with two independently
qualified adjacent phases uses z>=3 and requires support on both phases.
`t26o9` offered only the one low-z phase, so admission did not open the lower
threshold two-phase path. Good runs qualify multiple phases and/or stronger
resources. The code-level explanation is thus sparse/discarded U4 evidence
plus an unchanged independent confirmation gate, not a broken detector or proof
of GPU starvation. RF/interference/time variation remains unisolated without
paired IQ and detailed diagnostics. Offline replay success does not establish
live scheduling or RF equivalence.

## Implementation and constraints

[CODE-READ]

- `nr_csirs_disc_evidence_internal.h:10`: 256 bounded entries (matching the rejection-cache capacity), exact RE mapping,
  RB extent, period and phase keys. All 1024 ID scores are retained, never only
  selected winners. At most 32 fresh slots per evidence block; the next fresh
  group starts a new block. Gaps beyond 512 periods, clock rollback, ring/grid
  changes and explicit configuration epochs invalidate evidence. Geometry,
  antenna count and slot numerology cannot share evidence.
- `nr_csirs_disc_sweep.c:182`: the CPU reference forms slot-weighted averages
  from fresh slots only. Repeated calls on identical IQ return the same result
  with `fresh_slots=0`. The union fixture compares the complete cumulative
  result/moments with a separate sweep over the concatenated raw observations.
- `nr_csirs_disc_gpu_sweep.cu:104,274,310`: the GPU fuses the update into the
  existing averaging launch. Histories occupy an unused 256x1024-float tail
  of the **already allocated** capacity x160x1024-float score scratch buffer. No extra CUDA
  allocation, IQ transfer, score-vector download, or per-fresh-sweep kernel
  launch. Errors clear metadata before reuse. Wide delta searches invalidate
  the reused storage; production cumulative mode is limited to row 1, the
  complete 1024-ID sweep, delta_max=1, block_re=32, max_slots<=4 and ring capacity>=2.
- `nr_csirs_disc_gpu_sweep.cu:493`: opt-in kernel specialization is warmed before
  RX; flag-off keeps the original averaging specialization and launch sequence.
- `nr_csirs_disc.c:39,256,373,443`: reset epochs, never admit on cached-only IQ,
  and independently verify refined extents. A changed extent does not inherit
  coarse evidence. Up to 16 refined retry descriptions can replace ordinary
  hypotheses, at most one quarter of the existing per-pass limit, only while
  a matching footprint is still observed. They expire after 10000 ring writes.
- `ISAC_CSIRS_DISC_TRS_PRIORITY=1`: stable priority for row 1 with period>=80,
  preserving the builder's existing ordering within each class; no PCI, ID,
  frequency allocation, fixed extent, symbol or cell-specific phase prior.
  Remaining budget still examines other rows/periods. This flag does not change
  reject cooldowns or create additional feed windows.
- `nr_csirs_disc.h:98,163`: additive `fresh_slots` result and `evidence_epoch`
  configuration fields. Zero epoch preserves all existing score/gate semantics.
  Rebuild receiver and dynamically loaded modules together (struct ABI grows).

[ANALYTICAL] Live GPU allocation bytes and raw sample traffic are unchanged.
Per pass, full sequence evaluation remains <=32 hypotheses x1024 IDs x4 slots
by default, often less due to overlap removal. Accumulation adds at most
32x1024 float reads and writes (~256 KiB) **on device**, plus bounded host
metadata (about 12 KiB); it does not reread the IQ ring over the CPU bus. The CPU
reference lazily retains 1 MiB of score state per calling thread only when
explicitly enabled; the live GPU path does not allocate that reference state.
The result grows by four ABI bytes. This is bounded metadata/score bookkeeping,
not a claim of literally zero additional instructions or bytes. The ~2 GB/s
CPU-coherent traffic limit is why feed duty/ring capacity and FEP are untouched.
Actual kernel/CPU cost and total shared-memory traffic remain device gates.

## Gate mathematics and false-alarm scope

[ANALYTICAL] For a fixed geometry and ID j, let x(s,j) be the existing normalized
block/antenna score on unique slot s. T28 retains

```
Z_N(j) = sum_s x(s,j) / N
Z_(N+n)(j) = [N Z_N(j) + n Z_new(j)] / (N+n)
```

It then performs the **same** argmax and computes mu/sigma across the other
1023 IDs from this complete vector. Acceptance remains
`z>=max(2,mu+12*sigma)` and `z-z2>=4*sigma`; a WIN_Z override retains its existing
absolute margin and gate. There is no second relaxed gate, winner-only
Stouffer sum, repeated-IQ credit, or `sigma/sqrt(number_of_sweeps)` shortcut.
Persistent sequence sidelobes/common interference remain visible in the full
noise population instead of acquiring fictitious independent credit. Combining
scores is noncoherent over time; the per-block reference correlation remains
coherent. No stable inter-slot channel phase is assumed.

Under the **Gaussian, calibrated-null approximation** used to interpret the
noise-adaptive gate, a one-sided 12-sigma tail is Q(12)≈1.7765e-33 and a
1024-ID union bound per hypothesis/look is <=1.8192e-30. Across H hypotheses
and T scheduled looks it is <=1024 H T Q(12), without requiring independent
looks for that union bound; floor and ambiguity margin can only reject further.
T28 replaces a scheduled score/gate calculation, does not add a lower-threshold
OR branch, and keeps the work cap. Averaging independent noise reduces sigma
approximately as 1/sqrt(N), exposing stable weak pilots at the same standardized
gate. Correlation prevents that gain and is deliberately not divided away.

This is **not** an exact OTA false-alarm theorem: finite estimated moments,
Gold-code correlation, footprint selection and non-Gaussian interference violate
the idealized tail model; priority can also change the realized number of looks.
The 32-slot reset gives bounded evidence lifetime, not an anytime error guarantee
for an infinite run. Independent fresh sequence confirmation remains the arbiter;
it was not relaxed. Empirical calibration and live negative controls are still
required. Zero errors in a finite fixture cannot prove equal microscopic tails.
