## What it does (default off; fully blind, no cell constants)
Per RNTI the last 64 DCI 1_1 payloads are kept; each candidate layout (those the CRC evidence allows) is scored by its NDI/RV/HARQ-pid
readings (new tx with rv != 0, retransmission off the 0,2,3,1 order = violation; binomial test vs P0=0.15 refutes, vs q=0.5 verifies).
A layout is selected by lowest violation rate, a refuted solution is reselected (or the search reopens), a solution whose CRC passes were
all MCS 0 may seed the cell / other RNTIs only once HARQ-verified, and >= 3 RVRESCUEs of an RNTI force a re-score. Diagnostic added in
this session: `HQSCORE layout L: bad violations in n grants (kept|refuted)` at every reselection.

## Why the flag does not remove the rescues here [MEASURED, replay, mechanism NOT proven for OTA]
In on5 (tile3) RNTI 0x4ac6 had 20 RVRESCUE (dci_rv=1 ok_rv=0) while HQSCORE of its layout 905 stayed 0 violations in 49 grants (also at the
[rv rescues] re-scores), 0x4624 and 0x5264 likewise. So the rv=1 readings obey the HARQ rules (NDI toggle -> rv 0; retransmissions in
order 0,2,3,1): HARQ consistency cannot refute the layout, and no alternative candidate scores better. The one-bit-shift mechanism
(rv = [true rv LSB, pid MSB]) is therefore NOT what produces these rescues on the replay; whether it is on the OTA 255/473-line logs
is [HYPOTHESIS] (replay reproduces ~20 RVRESCUE per solved run, mostly one RNTI, vs 255/473 OTA). Layout 905 vs 903/886 (ant_bits 4 vs 5)
differ in other fields; several RNTIs end on 905 after starting on 903/886 with or without the flag.
Also noted: EVMGRANT pid= prints 0 for every grant of these RNTIs (the pid field of the grant, not the layout's 4 bits) -- the ring
reading and the grant reading are different views; a mismatch between them would itself be a bug to check (not done).
