### Hypotheses
1. Level / SSS floor: excluded (live level is higher; replay syncs unscaled with ISAC_SSS_REL_RATIO).
2. SSB period aliasing with the attempt cadence: excluded (period 20 ms).
3. CFO seed: -2400 vs measured -4881 leaves a 2.5 kHz residual; PSS correlation loss for 2.5 kHz at 30 kHz SCS is < 0.5 dB [INFERRED]. Not sufficient alone to
   lose >9 dB. Seed variant is still in the OTA list.
4. Fold smear (real defect, fixed below): the X410 reference error (~1.4 ppm, same oscillator for LO and sample clock) shifts the cell's 20 ms grid by ~100 samples
   per 0.6 s attempt; the replay hides it because tiled captures are exactly periodic. The EMA fold then lowers peak/avg by up to ~9 dB (acc is K times the
   per-capture level while only the newest bin holds the peak). It cannot explain failure of the FIRST attempt, so it is not the whole story [INFERRED].
5. Remaining candidate (unresolved): live signal at the PSS is weaker than in the 1011 capture (time/beam/location variation of the weakest cell), or something in
   the live ring path. Needs the new PSS_DECIM log line below.
