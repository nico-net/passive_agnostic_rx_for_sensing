## 1. Verdict on the stale drops (task 1)
**The PDSCH queue is not overloaded. Most jobs are already stale when they are enqueued; the lost time is spent upstream, in the PDCCH scan pool.**

* Rule [CODE-READ]: `nr_passive_samples_valid` (nr_passive_sample_lifetime.h): a job is valid while `producer_slot - job.absolute_slot < slots_per_frame - 2` = 18 slots = **9 ms** at 30 kHz, because the receive ring is indexed by slot-in-frame and overwritten one frame later. "Stale" = IQ ring age, not HARQ timing. The 9 ms is counted from the PDSCH slot, so the PDCCH scan-queue wait + the occasion's service time (candidate polar decodes, in-line work) are paid first; the PDSCH queue only sees the remainder. Stale drops are counted in `nr_pdsch_passive_queue.c` at dequeue (`queue_expired`), jobs of a slot group together.
* The PDSCH side is idle [M-OTA]: ring fill 0-1 % in nearly every `PDSCH_GATE` line (NV2: 0-18 %; VY2 shows 100 % once, during a freeze), 5 consumers busy only **9-26 %** (sum of stage wall totals / (5 x T): SV2 9 %, VY2 15 %, NV2 26 % with CPU demod), and 77-87 % of jobs wait < 100 us - 1 ms between enqueue and dequeue.
* Upper bound for "queue wait made them stale" [M-OTA, bound]: jobs with enqueue->dequeue wait >= 8 ms / stale drops = NV2 9 %, VY1 8 %, VB1 10 %, VB2 11 %, VY2 22 %, VA2 23 %, SV2 30 %, Vz1 35 %, VA1 37 %. So **63-92 % of stale drops were stale within < 8 ms of enqueue, i.e. born late.** The remaining 8-37 % are the freeze events (section 2): during them the 64-deep ring fills and everything behind it expires.
* Load model [M-OTA] (`stalls_data/ota_model.txt`, per run): arrival 360-820 jobs/s (replay GPUALLOC: ~500 jobs/s, so the OTA queue arrival rate is NOT higher), 1.5-2.8 bodies per decoded job, stage means 0.1-0.4 ms: PDSCH service capacity is >10x the arrival. Stale rate is 61-80 % in the first 30 s (acquisition, solver convergence, CSI-RS discovery) and a steady 25-38 % afterwards.
* What is saturated: the **PDCCH scan pool** [M-OTA]. Config `scan_thread="2:32:6"` = 2 consumers on cores 6 and 7, FIFO 50. `SCANTIME ded-only`: 228-244 k occasions/run (~1.8 k/s), p50 0.4-0.7 ms, p99 3-4 ms, 43-70 % of occasions > 500 us (`over500`), max 11-922 ms. `SCANPASS bank` runs 0.42-0.76 M x 218-407 us = 1.1-1.5 cores of the 2 (bank pass alone). Offered load ~0.6-0.9 per consumer, with bursts => queue wait of several ms is routine; `scanq` itself drops 3-6 % as stale (maxlag 28-32 slots). In windows where `scanq` stale/s is high, PDSCH stale/s is 1.5-3x higher (NV2 10-20 s window: scanq 557/s, pdschq 749/s). Scan core 7 is also PDSCH consumer 0's core (FIFO 40 < 50) and scan core 6 is the PUSCH core (`ISAC_PUSCH_CORES=6`) [CODE-READ + env].
* Why replays do not reproduce it [M-replay GPUALLOC/DECQUEUE, CODE-READ]: the replay cfg (`ue.passive.ota.chestfast.replay.cfg`) has **no UL monitor** (OTA: `ul_uci=1024:11:0:32`, `ulscan sched=18.5 M`, `dci01 rejects=18.5 M`, ~170 DCI RNTIs and a bank pass per RNTI), scan pool `2:32:10` (little cores, but nothing to scan), 12 s windows (no steady state) and no 0.9 s freezes. DECQUEUE's UL-monitor replays (`timul_replay.sh`) already show stale 1-27 % per run. Same arrival rate, far less scan work per occasion.
* [INF, not measured] The missing number is the lag at enqueue (scan wait + occasion service) split real/probe; it is not in any log. Needs a lag-at-enqueue histogram in `nr_pdsch_passive_queue_enqueue` (one counter array, a 10-line change) and a build.

Consequence for task 3: **EDF / "drop probes first" inside the PDSCH ring cannot help** (ring fill ~0 %, the ring never has a choice to make); the existing `ISAC_PDSCHQ_PROBE_LAST` already covers the full-ring case. The lever is upstream: (a) more/uncontended scan capacity (arm S1 below, no code), (b) when the scan queue has a backlog, serve newest-first or skip the PDSCH-job creation for occasions older than ~12 slots (a doomed job costs a candidate decode plus solver/probe state; needs the scan code, owner rr-csd-usc), (c) a longer sample lifetime (needs the producer-side ring, not a queue change). Not implemented.

Probe share [M-OTA, PDSCH_GATE]: VY2 probes admitted 22270 vs fulls 19429 of 58700 jobs; probes are not the capacity problem, they only add scan-side work per DCI (one DCI becomes up to ~10 solver-probe jobs for the same slot, e.g. rnti 0x45b3 abs 48761 in NV2).

## 2. The long stalls (task 2)
Three different things were mixed in "stall"; all [M-OTA] unless marked.
| class | evidence | status |
|---|---|---|
| A. whole-consumer freeze 0.86-0.92 s, once, early | NV2: scan consumer log gap utc +863 ms in the same slot, PDTIM fep/demod/ldpc max 877/882/882 ms (same PDTIM window, calls<=2000), pdschq max_lag 1621 slots (0.81 s), SCANTIME max 862 ms; Vz1: SCANTIME max 922 ms, max_lag 1855. Scan consumers AND PDSCH consumers freeze while the RT receive thread keeps running (`RFCENSUS`/`CHPOW` lines print on time, the gap sits right before "SIB1 decoded") | cause OPEN [INF: config-epoch/SIB1 handling or a lock held across consumers; not proven] |
| B. GPU-stage-only freezes 0.37-0.95 s, mid-run | VY2: fep 954 ms + chest 961 ms in one PDTIM window (calls 50200), demod 370 ms (calls 4801); SV2: demod 375 / 405 ms; scan max only 11.8 ms in those runs, so scan consumers were NOT frozen. Durations match the long CSI-RS discovery kernels in [M-nsys] (`sweep` up to 390 ms, `refine_kernel` 726 ms in gsq bA, `sweep` 228 ms in gal RL) | [INF] GPU contention with discovery; with `ISAC_GPU_RT_ISOLATE=1` (green contexts) the RL trace shows no RT-context kernel > 15 ms, so isolation may be incomplete on air or the cause differs. Not proven |
| C. 20-300 ms CPU-bound bodies in replays | DECTAIL: wall == CPU in PDTIMSLOW; [M-nsys RL/base/P2, 3 traces]: **passivePdsch threads have no CUDA API call > 4 ms** (except 1-2 launches), cudaMalloc max 16 ms, cudaHostAlloc max 39 ms and green-context creation 23 ms all at t < 1 s | allocation/cuFFT-plan/green-ctx/lazy-load suspects REJECTED for replays |

* Suspects rejected by data: runtime cudaMalloc/cudaHostAlloc/cuFFT plan creation/green-context creation/lazy kernel loading (all at start-up, <= 39 ms, `cuKernelGetName` max 1 ms) [M-nsys]. Remaining untested: page faults (`ISAC_MLOCK`), CPU frequency/thermal, logging bursts (run.log is 290 k lines per 150 s), a lock shared by scan + PDSCH consumers (class A). Disproving these needs a replay hit with `--trace=cuda,osrt,nvtx --cudabacktrace` and `perf sched`; blocked (no build/replay allowed, see header).
* Wall vs CPU time of the PDSCH stages is **not equal on air**: fep 125/150 us, chest 165/192, demod 215/251, ldpc 299/350 (cpu/wall, VY2); NV2 and SV2 show the same 11-17 % gap in every stage, also for the CPU-only demod of NV2 [M-OTA]. In replays slow bodies have wall == CPU [DECTAIL]. So on air each consumer loses ~15 % of its time off-CPU (preemption by the FIFO 97/50 threads on shared cores, IRQs - `irqaffinity=0-14` includes PDSCH cores 3,4,8,9 -, or blocking). That is the inflated per-stage sd (1-6 ms vs means 0.1-0.4 ms). Cause not separated.

## 3. Candidate OTA arm (no code; verify before any claim)
S1 = scan pool of three big-core consumers, with no PDSCH/PUSCH consumer on a scan core: cfg `tests/passive_rx/stl_scan3.cfg` (= `sd_t26ul.cfg` with `scan_thread="3:32:6"` -> cores 6,7,8) and extra env `ISAC_PDSCH_CORES=9,3,4,11,14 ISAC_PUSCH_CORES=2` on top of t26z (single line, replaces the two existing values). Rationale: scan work is the bottleneck and must stay on X925 cores (sd_t26ul.cfg: 6x slower on a little core); PDSCH consumers are only 9-26 % busy and already two of them run on little cores. Risk: PDSCH stages get ~1.3-1.5x slower on little cores [INF]; core 8 loses a PDSCH consumer; cores 17-19, 15, 16, 5 untouched.
