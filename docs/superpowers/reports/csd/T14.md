## Implementation and decisions

- `openair1/PHY/NR_UE_TRANSPORT/nr_csirs_blind_rt.c:240`: admission matches row, bitmap, both symbols,
  CDM, density, scrambling ID and slot correction. Nested verified extents and boundary deviations up to
  four RB reuse the same index; disjoint extents remain separate. Only a wider, independently re-swept
  incoming extent replaces the stored extent; it does not extrapolate an unverified union.
- `nr_csirs_blind_rt.c:254`: divisible periods are combined into the denser observation schedule;
  equivalent residues and a second adjacent row-1 burst phase reuse the existing candidate, including
  after confirmation. Every reused admission increments the atomic `csirs_disc_readmit_skipped` counter.
  The additive passive-metrics field is serialized when nonzero, preserving feature-off JSON bytes.
- Competing incompatible burst phases for the same physical geometry/ID and divisible period do not
  create clones. The stronger sweep wins while provisional; an independently confirmed burst takes
  precedence until an epoch reset. The dump contains weaker cross-lattice proposals for ID 415 at
  phases 20/21 as well as the stronger 15/16 burst. This selection is based on measured scores, not
  PCI, a resource catalogue, fixed RB coordinates or a deployment-specific period.
- `nr_csirs_blind_search.c:794`: discovery confirmation chooses the **largest primitive legal lattice
  explaining all independently scored hits**, with three distinct hits per phase. Two equally spaced
  phases of 2P are not a separate resource at 2P; row-1 TRS burst phases must be adjacent, including
  wraparound. Cumulative per-period evidence permanently rejects a period after its third residue,
  so a long fade cannot resurrect an alias by evicting earlier hits from the old eight-slot history.
  The seed controls sampling, not the exported period. Legacy NZP/ZP inference is unchanged.
- `nr_csirs_blind_rt.c:872`: the existing four-score budget selects the least-sampled **due phase**,
  reversing tie order on alternate rounds. Rotating by absolute slot starved resources; a fixed
  round-robin instead sampled some resources only every 2P/4P. Settled confirmations leave this
  budget; new seed information reopens probes. Missing a late phase during the first probes cannot
  strand it, and a confirmed super-period remains under observation after a denser admission.
- `nr_csirs_blind_rt.c:1189`: a row-1 candidate with two independently swept adjacent phases requires
  three fresh z >= 3 hits **on each phase**. Single-phase candidates and all other rows retain z >= 4.
  The blanket z >= 3 experiment admitted the known row-4/ID-418 coherent distractor and was rejected.
  Without the burst-specific gate, ID 582 never got a hit despite repeated sweep winners at z=3.3–3.5.
  The final dump confirms the weak TRS resources and leaves that row-4 distractor unconfirmed.
- `nr_csirs_blind_rt.c:704`: publication compares the full previously exported candidate and lattice,
  removes old monitor entries before adding the refined extent/period/phases, and updates the observer
  identity without incrementing its confirmation count for the same resource. Discovery reset removes
  owned monitor entries. Monitor remove/add and rate-match reads remain serialized by the existing lock.

The discovery contract `nr_csirs_disc.h` is unchanged. Added fields/functions in the blind-search,
observer and metrics interfaces do not alter legacy feature-off behavior. No DL GPU, grantwork,
`nr_td_*`, `radio/USRP/*`, or frozen sens6 sources were edited.

## Limitations and DEFERRED MEASUREMENTS

[IMPLEMENTED, NOT OTA VALIDATED] Same-ID/same-geometry overlapping resources with genuinely independent,
incompatible bursts cannot be distinguished by this canonical physical identity: provisional competition
retains the strongest burst and confirmed identity wins until reset. Row-1 two-phase discovery assumes
an adjacent TRS burst. A sparse capture may temporarily support a longer period until denser admissions
and fresh hits falsify it. The weak-burst confirmation gate has recorded-grid/synthetic coverage, not an
OTA false-positive guarantee. DL-actor `confirm_wait_max_us`, X410 stability and live slots-to-confirm
must be measured by the orchestrator.
