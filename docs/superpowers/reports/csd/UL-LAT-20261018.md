Causes, in order of size:
1. **Delay estimation.** perf [b, BGDISC run, n=1]: nr_passive_est_delay = 5.3 % of 9.7 % (decode_inner), i.e. ~55 % of a decode
   call's CPU. One 4096-point IDFT (plus scaling and two 4096 scans) per antenna x DM-RS symbol, even for a 3-PRB grant (36 bins).
2. **UL_RETRY on noise.** ISAC_UL_RETRY_SNR_DB is unset in every env (its comment says snr_db was not populated; it is now), so every
   CRC-failed grant <= 12 PRB was decoded 3 times. b is mostly 3-PRB grants: mean 1.73 calls/grant, 3 in the tail.
3. **LDPC on hopeless TBs.** Every noise TB runs 8 LDPC iterations: GPU LDPC wall ~2.5 ms per 12-CB grant on tim (pool shared
   with DL), HARQ-combined retransmissions go to the CPU decoder (the GPU pool refuses them).
4. **UCI footprint search** after a CRC fail: up to ISAC_UL_UCI_BUDGET=8 more demux + decode passes; in the tim tail after (3)
   the dec stage was still ~2.5 ms on 200-PRB grants.
5. **Scheduling.** The consumers run SCHED_OTHER (ISAC_PUSCH_PRIO=0) on the little cores 0/1 (A725, 2.8 GHz), with 24 unpinned
   receiver threads allowed on 0-16 [replay, ps]. Tail wall exceeded cpu by 1-3 ms.
- Queue wait was not the driver: arr2deq median 0.05-0.2 ms, tail <= ~1 ms after (1)-(2). No drops (PUSCHQ2 full=0).
- BGDISC/NID_BG: FEP stage (where the inline search used to be) is 0.17-0.25 ms mean, flat; NID_BG CPU unchanged (UL-REALDEC).

## 2. Levers (all default off)
| flag | what | where |
|---|---|---|
| `ISAC_UL_DELAY_FAST=1` | Pruned delay estimator for an LS row whose support is <= n/16 bins (<= 21 PRB at 4096, <= 4 PRB at 1536): 8x-oversampled float FFT, exact double refinement around coarse samples >= cos^2(pi m/2np) Cmax (Bernstein bound), Parseval for the peak/mean test. Same decision as the full n-point grid. Falls back to the full IDFT otherwise. | nr_passive_delay_fast.h, nr_ul_channel_estimation.c |
| `ISAC_UL_DELAY_ONCE=1` | Delay estimated on the grant's first DM-RS symbol per antenna, reused on the later DM-RS symbols (key: layer, start subcarrier, PRB count, slot). | nr_ul_channel_estimation.c |
| `ISAC_UL_RETRY_SNR_MARGIN=3` | UL_RETRY alternates only if DM-RS SNR >= 10 log10(2^(Qm R) - 1) - 3 dB. The alternates never change the DM-RS, so the SNR holds under every alternate. | nr_pusch_passive_queue.c |
| `ISAC_UL_LDPC_SNR_MARGIN=6` | A TB whose DM-RS SNR is > 6 dB below that bound (12 dB for a HARQ-combined retx) runs 1 LDPC iteration and skips the UCI footprint search. Rate recovery and the HARQ soft buffer are unchanged. Counter: UL_LDPC_SNR_GATE capped=. | nr_pusch_passive_decode.c |
| (rejected) `ISAC_PUSCH_PRIO=20 ISAC_UL_SNAP_PRIO=21` | Existing knobs: UL consumers SCHED_FIFO 20 (t26gE sets 0). Cuts ~0.5 ms off the p99 but stalls (section 4). Not in t26uL. | env only |

## Risks / open
- Replays only: both captures are noise in the UL slots, so the LDPC gate fires on 76-87 % of the
  decodes (capped 2240-2770 per 90 s run). On air with decodable UEs they fire less, and the cost per grant is the full decode.
  The tail is then the wide-grant rx_pusch on the little cores (~1.7-2.1 ms for 200 PRB, 4 RX).
- SNR gates trust the DM-RS SNR estimate (nr_ulsch_dmrs_snr_db). A wrong layout/id hypothesis has low DM-RS SNR and cannot
  pass CRC anyway. A biased-low estimate on a real UE would cost CRC: margins 3/6 dB (+6 dB for combined retx) are the guard.
  rfsim A2 (34-37 dB) does not exercise the gates; a low-SNR rfsim arm would.
- DELAY_FAST/ONCE: float vs fixed-point can flip a peak/mean ratio within rounding of 15. ONCE assumes one arrival time per
  grant (true for one UE in one slot).
- Remaining tail (t26uL): wide (~200 PRB) grants, rx_pusch ~1.7-2.1 ms CPU on the little cores 0/1, plus ~1.5-2 ms
  off-CPU (SCHED_OTHER consumers share cores 0/1 with 24 unpinned receiver threads). Next levers: UL consumers on big X925
  cores (needs the OTA core plan), or keep the floating threads off the UL cores; the GPU pass once ids are confirmed.
