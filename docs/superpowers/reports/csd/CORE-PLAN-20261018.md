# CORE-PLAN-20261018: thread map, fewer cores, and the UL consumers on big cores

Branch csd/int16-cores (from csd/int16-int 5e8320830f). Host: DGX GB10 (big X925 cores 5-9 and 15-19, little A725 cores 0-4 and 10-14;
isolcpus/nohz_full 15-19). Labels: [MEASURED, DGX replay, capture, n], [MEASURED, DGX rfsim], [CODE-READ], [INFERRED].
Captures are never pooled. b = raw_pci415_12s_b (Vodafone), tim = raw_pci923_tim_12s_tile3 (TIM).

## Answer

**Strict gate: not passed. Recommended for the on-air A/B: t26kE = t26uL + plan K5 (16 cores), with plan K (14 cores) as the
fewest-core alternative.** All numbers are replay [MEASURED, DGX replay, b / tim, interleaved with t26uL]. Nothing was run on air.

- **UL p99 halves when the PUSCH consumers get big cores of their own** (K5: PUSCH on 8, 9, with no other critical thread on
  those cores). b 3204 -> 1700 us and tim 3881 -> 1753 us (n=3 each, g8). K3, which uses the same PUSCH placement, gives
  b 3304 -> 1931 (n=6) and tim 4063 -> 1735 (n=3). UL p50 drops by 15-30 %. UL drops are 0.
- **DL p99 is 2-15 % worse in every arm that uses the float lane.** K5: b 3163 -> 3212 (+2 %), tim 2261 -> 2418 (+7 %); DL p50
  +9-14 %. So "DL p99 not worse" fails, but the cost stays inside the user's "~10 % worse for saved cores" rule. The lane-only arm F
  shows the same DL p50 rise on tim (952 -> 1026 us, n=3): the cost comes from packing the background threads, not from the UL
  consumers. The mechanism is not isolated. A wider lane (2,10-14, arm K4) brought tim DL p99 back to L's level (2298 vs 2310, n=3).
- crc_q, n_real and drops stay inside the t26uL spread for K, K4 and K5. Exception: one tim run per arm in L and in K lost its
  DL solver lock (n_real 32 / 49). That happened in both arms, so it is replay variance, not the plan.
- **Placing PDSCH consumers inside the lane is unsafe. That rules out K2/K3/K3w (PDSCH consumers on lane cores 13, 14).**
  4 of 15 runs in those arms had stalls: 69 ms, 136 ms, and two of ~937 ms, all in UL GPU FEP launches. One of them had DL
  drops of 4.6 % and UL drops of 6.4 %. The other arms had 0 stalls > 50 ms in 39 runs, t26uL included.
  [INFERRED] mechanism: an RT 40 consumer spins inside a CUDA call on a core where the SCHED_OTHER lane thread that holds the
  CUDA lock cannot run, until RT throttling (sched_rt_runtime 950 ms) lets it run.
  It is the same priority inversion as UL-LAT's FIFO stall, and it explains why t26uC (PUSCH on 5 = UEthread_0 and 6 = scan) cost DL on air.
- Rule set, in general form:
  1. Every critical consumer (scan, PDSCH, PUSCH, UEthread_0) gets a core that is not shared with a thread of another class:
     RT spinners and SCHED_OTHER CUDA users never share a core.
  2. UL consumers go on big cores.
  3. Everything not pinned goes into one float lane on little cores, `ISAC_FLOAT_CORES`.
- **Cores on air (union of all receiver masks, DPDK 17-19 not counted):**
  - t26uL: 17 (0-16).
  - K5 (t26kE): 16 (0,1,3-16; core 2 is free).
  - K: 14 (3-16; 0, 1, 2 free). K shares PUSCH 8, 9 with PDSCH consumers 1, 2 (RT 40), so it breaks rule 1. It had no stall in
    12 runs, but on tim its UL gain is smaller (p99 3.7-4.1 -> 2.7-3.6 ms) and DL p99 is +9.5 % (median of 6).
- UL correctness: rfsim A2 is 100.0 % with the K flags (5423/5423) and with the K3 flags on the final binary (5425/5425)
  [MEASURED, DGX rfsim A2, n=1 each].

## 1. How the cores are assigned today (t26uL, on air)

[CODE-READ] starttest.sh, ota.sh, t26uL.env, sd_t26ul.cfg, nr_core_plan.c, system.c threadCreate():
- `ISAC_CORE_PLAN=0` in the env. The automatic planner (nr_core_plan.c) is therefore off. It would also move every thread to its
  background CPUs and change policies, so it is not a drop-in fix.
- Pinned by env or cfg: UEthread_0 on 5 (`ISAC_UE_RT_CORE=5`, prepended by starttest.sh, RR 97); scan consumers
  passivePdcch0/1 on 6, 7 (cfg `scan_thread "2:32:6"`, RR 50); PDSCH consumers on 7, 8, 9, 3, 4 (RR 40);
  PUSCH consumers on 0, 1 and the UL snapshot on 0 (SCHED_OTHER); dmrsScan on 2; gpuFepWorker on 10; csirsDisc on 11 with detect
  12-14; pdcchUssHash on 12; nID GPU sweep (unnamed `nr-uesoftmodem` thread) on 13; thread pool Tpool on 8, 9, 15 (RR 97, idle in
  these runs); uhd_ctrl on 15; usrpRing on 16 (FIFO 95); DPDK lcores 17-19.
- Unpinned: about 25 threads take the inherited mask. On air (sudo, isolcpus) that mask is 0-14; in replay heavy.sh sets 0-16.
  These are pdcchJanon, ulBgDisc, pdschRecov, the 4 DL__actor + 2 UL__actor + SYNC__actor (RR 97), the ITTI tasks
  (RR 50), logAsync, pdcp_timer, the CUDA helper threads, the main thread, dmrsIdBg/gpuCheckBg (own list 0-4,10-14).
  The UL NID background worker is created by a PUSCH consumer, so it inherits that consumer's core and runs there at SCHED_IDLE.
- Replay vs air: replay reads the raw IQ file inside UEthread_0 (radio/COMMON/raw_iq.c has no thread of its own) on core 5 (~52 %
  of a core). On air there is no file read. UEthread_0 instead copies from the usrpRing producer (core 16, FIFO 95; ~39 % on air
  [MEASURED, DGX OTA, SYSAN-20261015]), while uhd_ctrl busy-polls on 15 (~64 % on air) and DPDK takes 17-19. Cores 15-16 carry no load
  in the replay. UL-LAT's "raw-IQ reader on core 6" is not what the code does: core 6 is passivePdcch0. That report's
  core-6 arms therefore shared the scan core, not a reader core.

Wakeups are high only for the timer-driven threads (scan2Slow ~980/s, pdcp_timer 1000/s, time source 1000/s, actors ~240-480/s each,

Findings:
- In L the two heaviest floaters run on the big PDSCH cores: pdcchJanon 38 % on 6/8/9 (b) and ulBgDisc 45 % on 8/9 (tim).
  On tim the CPU-bound helpers sat at 92 % each on 8 and 9 in an earlier sample [MEASURED, DGX replay tim, t26uL, n=1, other agent's binary].
- The RR 97 actors (DL__actor/UL__actor, ~1900 wakeups/s together) float over every core, including the scan and PDSCH cores.
- Why t26uC cost DL on air [INFERRED from the code and this map]: C put the SCHED_OTHER PUSCH consumers on 5 (UEthread_0, RR 97)
  and 6 (passivePdcch0, RR 50). Those are not idle cores but the two RT-hogged ones. The consumers are preempted there while
  they hold locks shared with the DL path (GPU pools, CUDA launches): the same priority inversion UL-LAT saw with FIFO consumers.

## 3. Mechanism (commit 8730c2cbf9 + follow-up, default off)

`ISAC_FLOAT_CORES=<cpu list>` (only with `ISAC_CORE_PLAN=0`; ignored otherwise), in the existing core-plan helper
(common/utils/nr_core_plan.c), no new per-thread variables:
- main() moves every task that still has the inherited mask to the list. Pinned tasks keep their core. Every thread created later
  inherits the list (janon reads the process mask; ulBgDisc, pdschRecov, actors, CUDA/log helpers inherit it).
- threadCreate()/nr_core_plan_create() with core -1 use the list even when the creator is pinned.
- The UL NID background worker enters the lane instead of its PUSCH consumer's core (nr_ul_nid_bg.c, one line).
- The rest is env only: `ISAC_DMRS_SCAN_CORE=-1` (dmrsScan into the lane), `ISAC_GPU_CHECK_CORES=10-14` (dmrsIdBg's own list).
- Unit test FloatCores.UnpinnedThreadsGoToTheLaneAndPinsAreKept; test_nr_core_plan 18/18 [MEASURED, DGX unit test].
- No PDCCH scan internals touched (nr_pdcch_blind_monitor*.c unchanged). No SCHED_FIFO/RR added.
- Tool: tests/passive_rx/sysan_threads.py now records allowed CPUs, wakeups/s, involuntary switches/s, run-queue wait and a
  per-core thread list, and can start its window at a log regex (used here at "Initial sync successful").

## 7. Risks on air

- Replay is not air:
  - Cores 15-16 are idle in replay; on air uhd_ctrl busy-polls core 15 (~64 %) and the ring producer runs on 16.
  - UEthread_0 reads the file in replay; on air it copies from the ring.
  - On air the PDSCH consumers are ~25 % busy each at daytime load (SYSAN-20261015), against 3-17 % in replay. K5 moves PDSCH
    consumers 1 and 2 from big cores 8, 9 to little cores 0, 1. A similar move (PDSCH off core 7) was rejected on air on
    2026-10-17 (drops 1.1 / 2.9 %). That is the main DL risk of t26kE: watch drops and DL p99 per cell.
  - Run-queue and wakeup behaviour in the lane under daytime discovery load is unmeasured: the sampler on air will show it.
- `--thread-pool 8,9,15` (starttest TPOOL default) keeps idle RR 97 Tpool workers pinned on 8, 9, now the PUSCH cores. They were
  idle in every sample. `TPOOL=15` would remove them but was not tested.
- gpuFepWorker (RR 40, core 10) and the RR 97 actors live in the lane next to SCHED_OTHER CUDA users. That is the same pairing as
  the stall mechanism above. They were idle or short-running here (<= 7 % together), and no stall appeared in the lane-only arms.
- SCHED_FIFO/RR was not added anywhere, nothing touches 17-19, and ISAC_RX_BRANCH_FO is unset.
- The PDCCH scan internals are untouched (nr_pdcch_blind_monitor*.c not in the diff).
- How to check on air:
  - run `python3 tests/passive_rx/sysan_threads.py /home/nicola/ OUT.json 90 40` in each run: every unpinned thread must show
    allowed=10-14, and passivePusch0/1 must be on 8/9 with low rqwait;
  - grep `ULSTALL` (> 50 ms) and `FLOAT_CORES` in run.log.
