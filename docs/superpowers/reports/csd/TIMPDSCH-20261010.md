# TIM-PDSCH (2026-10-10): TIM PCI 923 PDSCH solved by a blind per-UE DM-RS identity scan

Host: DGX GB10 (aarch64), worktree `rr-csd-timp`, branch `csd/tim-pdsch` (from `csd/otatest` 06b63eaba6).
Every build, test and replay went through `heavy.sh`, one at a time. No radio. Replays: `tests/passive_rx/timacq_replay.sh`,
`ENVFILE=ota/t26p.env` (includes `ISAC_PDCCH_POINTA_REF=1`), speed 0.25, 4 RX. TIM = `raw_pci923_tim_12s_tile3`
(capture centre 3731.91 MHz, `-C 3759990000 --ssb 233 --initial-fo -4800`, CAP_TICK 195322029, TIM arms add
`ISAC_CSIRS_DISC=0 ISAC_CSIRS_FULL=0`). Vodafone = PCI 415, `-C 3680010000 --ssb 1171 --initial-fo 6900`, capture
**b** = `raw_pci415_12s_b` (CAP_TICK 208029025) and **8** = `raw_pci415_12s_20261008` (CAP_TICK 210036277).
Counts come from `tests/passive_rx/timpdsch_stats.py` and the last `PDSCHQ queued=` line. Results per cell and per capture are never merged.

## Root cause

- **[MEASURED, replay TIM]** TIM gives each UE its own PDSCH DM-RS `scramblingID0`, and the data scrambling identity is the
  PCI (`dataScramblingIdentityPDSCH` absent). Every RNTI that was solved used the hypothesis (DM-RS id = blind per-UE id, data id = 923):
  0x5264 -> 1798, 0x4ac6 -> 298, 0x4624 -> 781, 0x5214 -> 1743, 0x516a -> 634 (from the `SOLVED ... scr=` field, which is decoded as
  id | (data+1) << 16). The scan also found 0x4e32 -> 1677, 0x5954 -> 272 and 0x5098 -> 638, which agree with the earlier TIMORC oracle map.
  Most of these identities are above 1023. The existing estimator searches 0..1023 per CORESET, and it assumes DM-RS id = data id. On TIM it
  "decided" a noise maximum (88). So no single CORESET identity can be right on this cell, and before this change every TIM hypothesis failed CRC.
- **[CODE-READ]** The pilot sequence of the scan uses TS 38.211 7.4.1.1.1 referenced to CRB 0, and the scan is scored on the absolute CRBs
  of the carrier. With `ISAC_PDCCH_POINTA_REF=1` the PDCCH is acquired on TIM. Without it nothing is solved
  (**[MEASURED, replay TIM]** earlier `c3_tim_p_a`: 0 solved).
- Ruled out by the solved decodes: the BWP/RIV, the TDRA (`S[tda 0]`, layouts 886/903/905) and the rate matching are right for those RNTIs.
- **[HYPOTHESIS]** The remaining unsolved RNTIs are SNR-limited. 0x4e32 has its id 1677 voted on 21-215 slots, and the filter confirms its
  DM-RS on the layouts, yet it gives 0 CRC-OK over ~2.4-2.7k decodes. It is scheduled at AL1, which points to a high MCS that the gNB chose
  for a strong link, while this receiver sees TIM 11-14 dB weaker. The MCS was not logged.

## Fix (default off: `ISAC_PDSCH_DMRS_ID_SCAN=<full scans per s of signal>`, 1 = 8/s; validated at 16)

- `nr_pdsch_dmrs_scan.[ch]`: a decode-free scan over the full 16-bit N_ID x n_SCID space, using the same-k' coherence of
  type-1 CDM group 0 (it is robust to 2-port OCC). The x2 Gold register is linear in c_init, so each candidate is an XOR of 31 basis rows.
  Energy chunks of up to 16 RB are scanned. A cell-wide pool lets the slots between full scans be re-tested cheaply.
  Each slot votes for every C-RNTI that has a PDSCH in it, and each RNTI's top-2 identities become solver hypotheses.
- `nr_pdsch_passive_queue.c`: runs the rate-limited scan on the DM-RS symbol of unsolved C-RNTI grants. RNTIs that are already decided
  keep voting through the pool/cache but get no more full scans, so an early wrong lead can still be overtaken (seen: 0x4ac6 -> 1677).
  It logs `DMRS_SCAN ids` whenever an RNTI's ranking changes.
- `nr_pdcch_blind_monitor_rt.c` / `nr_pdsch_solver.[ch]`: for **unsolved** RNTIs only, the own ids (first as (id, PCI), then (id, id)) replace
  the CORESET's ids, and layout readings whose PRBs do not carry the RNTI's DM-RS are dropped. Without the gate on solved RNTIs,
  capture 8 lost CRC-OK decodes (355 vs 611, run g_vf8_on).
- Earlier commit d0c6c0bc8e: a GPU exact chest NULL-entry SIGSEGV that `t26p` triggered.

## Results

**[OFFLINE VERIFIED, DGX unit test]** `test_nr_pdsch_dmrs_scan` 4/4 passed, and `test_nr_pdsch_solver` 13/13 passed.

**[MEASURED, replay TIM PCI 923, t26p, final binary unless noted]**

| run | scan | solved | RNTIs with CRC-OK full decodes | PDSCHQ crc_ok | crash |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| f_tim_off_a | off | 0 | 0 | - | no |
| h_tim_on_a | 16 | 4 | 4 (0x5264 183/278, 0x4624 12/27, 0x4ac6 12/66, 0x5214 11/15) | 218 | no |

| f_tim_on_a / f_tim_on_b (before the decided-RNTI fix) | 16 | 6 / 4 | 5 / 2 | - | no |

Scan cost (TIM): 19-22 full scans of ~63-67 ms each, plus ~1k pool checks of ~0.1 ms each, over 12 s of signal (~0.12 core on average, in bursts).
