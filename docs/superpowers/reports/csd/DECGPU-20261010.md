# DECGPU — per-grant PDSCH decode time on the GB10 (2026-10-10/11)

Branch `csd/dec-gpu` (worktree `rr-csd-dtg`, base `csd/otatest` 2181f7506f). Host: DGX Spark GB10 (sm_121, 48 SMs,
CUDA 13.0, unified memory). Every build, test and replay ran through `heavy.sh`; no radio, OTA, capture or UHD tool.
All replay numbers are offline: `tests/passive_rx/timacq_replay.sh` at 0.25x on `raw_pci415_12s_b` ("b",
CAP_TICK 208029025) and `raw_pci415_12s_20261008` ("old", CAP_TICK 210036277), `-C 3680010000 --ssb 1171
--initial-fo 6900`, env = `ota/t26q.env` (single-line version, md5 9fca9980..., frozen copy used by every arm) +
`ISAC_PDTIM_PCT=1`. Commits: c41570ddc2, 18726ef96f, 2bdfdcdf31.

## Verdict

* **Goal (< 0.5 ms per decode, sum of stage means and p50): p50 met, mean not met.** `[MEASURED, DGX GB10 replay b]`
  - per-decode p50 of fep+chest+demod+ldpc: 455-475 us with the new flags, 465-495 us at baseline (already under 0.5 ms);
  - sum of stage means: **954 -> 828 us** (b, 2 runs per arm), demod mean **397 -> 291 us**, demod p99 **6.6 -> 4.0 ms**.
  - The means are made by tails (per-decode p99 7-13 ms). The nsys trace shows why: the CSI-RS discovery kernels take
    ~70 % of the GPU and run for 10-600 ms each, and the PDSCH kernels (median 1-40 us) wait for SMs behind them for
    up to 4-70 ms (see Profile). The remaining gap to 0.5 ms is GPU sharing, not the PDSCH arithmetic.
* **Bit-exactness: met for everything moved.** No CRC can change by construction:
  - Nl = 3, 4 demodulation on the GPU: **16,000 / 16,000** grants equal to the CPU (`ISAC_PDSCH_GPU_EXACT_DEMOD_CHECK=1`,
    every grant: LLRs, comp, mags, dl_valid_re, log2_maxh, branch, EVM within tolerance). `[MEASURED, replay b]`
  - GPU LDPC (port of OAI's decoder): **0 mismatches** in `test_nr_pdsch_gpu_ldpc` (2,556 per-iteration hard-decision
    snapshots and 413 CRC early-stop cases vs `./libldpc.so` `LDPCdecoder`, BG1/BG2, all rate row sets, Z 6-384,
    noise from hopeless to clean incl. -128/127 saturation) `[OFFLINE VERIFIED]`; in the receiver **22,000 / 22,000**
    TBs equal to the CPU decoder (c bytes + every code block's success flag, `ISAC_PDSCH_GPU_EXACT_LDPC_CHECK=1`).
    `[MEASURED, replay b]`
* **GPU LDPC: exact, faster standalone, slower in the receiver -> left off.** Standalone (idle GPU) a TB of 1-4 BG1
  Z=384 code blocks decodes in 114-125 us (CPU 135-650 us); in the receiver the same kernel waits behind the discovery
  kernels: 331 us per TB (PDTIM ldpc mean 381 vs 234-272 us on the CPU). It becomes worth enabling once the PDSCH work
  has protected SMs (next step 1).
* **CPU-side LDPC with identical CRC:** nothing found that keeps per-CB results identical and is cheaper: the CPU
  decoder is OAI's flooding min-sum (not layered), early stop is already its CRC check from iteration 3, and the inline
  pool decodes the 1.5-1.9 code blocks per TB sequentially. Parallel code-block decoding is a scheduling change (the
  `rr-csd-dtq` side). `[CODE-READ]`

## 1. Profile (task 1)

PDTIM per decode, baseline (A1, A2, b) `[MEASURED]`: fep 120-130, chest 175-182, demod 397, ldpc 234-272 us; p50 of
the sum 465-495 us, p99 9.6-11.7 ms. Per stage the p50s are small (fep 75, chest 75, demod 145, ldpc 135-165) and the
p99s are milliseconds (fep 1.3-1.4, chest 2.4-3.0, demod 6.4-6.8 ms).

**Demod (397 us mean).** Three contributors:
1. Nl >= 3 grants (3-4 % of grants, 82 % of them QPSK) were refused by the GPU path and ran the CPU demodulation at
   ~1.45 ms each: ~140 us of the 397 us mean. Fixed (section 2.1).
2. GPU time per grant, by kernel, with `ISAC_PDSCH_GPU_EXACT_DEMOD_PROF=1` (receiver, all kernels serialised; NP run):
   gather 31, level+decide 25, mask/pw 13, ALIGN 21, **EVM 115** (evm1 38, evm2 33, evm3 44), comp 15, MMSE 17,
   kappa 2, LLR 10 us. The EVM chain (diagnostic sums for ISAC_PDSCH_EVM_GRANT / the SNR gate, not LLR inputs) runs on
   a second stream but the LDPC waited for it: it was the critical path. Fixed (section 2.3).
3. The tail (p99 4-7 ms, max 50-175 ms per grant) is GPU waiting: `cpu == wall` in PDTIM (cudaStreamQuery spin).

**nsys (`-t cuda`, replay b, E arm)** `[MEASURED]`: GPU time by kernel family over the run: CSI-RS discovery
(`resident::segment` 22 %, `refine_kernel` 20 %, `energies` 11 %, `sweep` 8 + 3 %, `resident::finish` 6 %,
`lattice_screen` 3 %, `score`/`standardize` 5 %) ~ 70 %; PDSCH exact FEP / delay / chest / demod ~ 15 %. Kernel
durations: `refine_kernel` median 5.6 ms, max 623 ms; `resident::segment` max 118 ms; `resident::finish` max 45 ms.
PDSCH kernels: `k_gather` median 5.3 us / max 6.4 ms, `k_comp` 2.3 us / 4.2 ms, `k_llr` 1.4 us / 4.5 ms, `k_fir`
8.6 us / 70 ms, `k_fep_exact` 37 us / 7.5 ms, `k_delay_exact` 30 us / 6.1 ms. A small kernel lasting milliseconds is
a kernel whose blocks wait for SMs: the discovery kernels' long-running blocks hold them (stream priority only orders
pending blocks, it does not preempt). `ISAC_GPU_DISCOVERY_BUDGET_PCT=5` instead of 30 did not change the picture
(EB5: sum of means 899, p99 9.3 ms).

**LDPC (234-272 us mean).** 1.6-1.9 code blocks per TB; PDTIM ldpc p50 135-165 us, p99 1.1-1.3 ms; one TB decode per
decode (ldpc_tb = decodes: no LBRM/RV retries in this env). CPU decoder on an idle core (ldpctest, BG1 Z=384): 117-165 us
per code block (7-9 iterations). The stage also holds descrambling and LLR normalisation.

## 2. Changes (all default off; flag off = previous behaviour)

### 2.1 Nl = 3, 4 on the GPU — `ISAC_PDSCH_GPU_EXACT_DEMOD_NL34=1` (c41570ddc2)
`nr_dlsch_mmse()` for 3 and 4 layers in `nr_pdsch_gpu_demod.cu`:
* QAM-FIX mode 2 (`k_mmse2n<NL>`): the generic Gauss-Jordan of the CPU source with the operation order already proved
  for Nl = 2 (pivot by `cabs`, `cmul(M[k][c], inv)`, `M[r][c] - cmul(M[k][c], f)`, libgcc `__divdc3`);
* the stock fixed-point MMSE (`k_mmse0n<NL>`, every Nl >= 3 QPSK grant): `mult_cpx_conj_vector` sums with adds_epi16,
  the `add_epi32` diagonal loading of nvar >> 3 on the (re | im << 16) word, `nr_determin()`'s cofactor recursion along
  index 0 with `mult_complex_vectors`' truncating madd/srai/slli lanes, the adjugate, the ZF products, and the
  det-based thresholds (`mulhrs`).
Host: `refuse()` resolves `qam_mode` exactly as `nr_rx_pdsch()` (Nl >= 3 with QPSK or mode 1 -> 0) and passes `nvar`.
Check: 16,000 / 16,000 equal (Nchk). Effect (b, N vs A, CHECK=64): demod 397 -> 315-363 us, refused Nl = 0.

### 2.2 GPU LDPC, bit-exact — `ISAC_PDSCH_GPU_EXACT_LDPC=1` [`_CHECK=N`] (18726ef96f)
`nr_pdsch_gpu_ldpc.{cu,h}` (in `libpdsch_gpu.so`), `nr_pdsch_gpu_ldpc_host.{c,h}`, one hook in
`passive_ldpc_decode_core()` (`nr_pdsch_gpu_ldpc_decode(&slot_parameters, tb_dec)`, = `tb_dec()` when off).
* Same arithmetic as `nrLDPC_decoder.c` (aarch64 128-bit path), per edge: two-pass CN (abs_epi8 with |-128| = 128 read
  unsigned, min_epu8/max_epu8 min1/min2, cmpeq tie rule, cap 127, xor sign), BN int16 sum saturated to int8, subs_epi8
  extrinsics; plus the one non-obvious rule found by the test: `nrLDPC_bn2cnProcBuf_BG1/BG2` never refresh the last BN
  of every CN group other than 19 (BG1) / 8, 10 (BG2) — the single-edge parity column — so that BN->CN message stays
  the channel LLR forever (differs from `sat(llr+c2v)-c2v` only on saturation, enough to flip bits).
* Stop rule as OAI: hard decisions packed MSB-first after iterations 3 .. max+1 (where `LDPCdecoder` calls
  `check_crc`); the host takes the first snapshot whose `check_crc()` passes; none = failure (c zeroed, abort set, so
  later code blocks fail as with the inline pool). The GPU stops early only at a zero syndrome; if that snapshot's CRC
  fails the TB is redone by the CPU decoder (never seen: 0 in 22,000 TBs).
* Rate dematching / deinterleaving / filler / int8 packing: the segment decoder's code on the CPU (refuses HARQ
  combining into a kept buffer).
* Kernel: one 8-block thread-block cluster (8 x 512 threads) per code block, cluster barriers, 4 lifting indices per
  thread with SIMD-in-word intrinsics for Z % 4 == 0 (scalar kernel otherwise), branch-free batched loads.
  Standalone `[MEASURED, DGX GB10 test_nr_pdsch_gpu_ldpc, idle GPU]`, BG1 Z=384 R=1/3, 8 iterations:

  | code blocks / launch | GPU decodable | GPU hopeless | CPU (LDPCdecoder, sequential) |
  |---:|---:|---:|---:|
  | 1 | 114 us | 118 us | 135-164 us |
  | 2 | 116 us | 120 us | 256-327 us |
  | 4 | 123 us | 125 us | 507-655 us |

  In the receiver: gpu 331 us / TB, PDTIM ldpc 381 us (L1) vs 234-272 us on the CPU — slower, see Verdict.
* `ISAC_PDTIM_PCT=1` (same commit): 10-us histograms per PDTIM stage and of the per-decode sum, printed as `PDTIMPCT`
  p50/p90/p99 with the PDTIM line (`gpudemod_stats.py` prints it).

### 2.3 EVM sums off the critical path — `ISAC_PDSCH_GPU_EXACT_DEMOD_EVM_DEFER=1` (2bdfdcdf31)
The demodulation returns when the main stream (LLRs, comp, decision, kappa) is done; the EVM chain keeps running on
its stream and its partial sums are folded into `nr_pdsch_evm_acc` after the LDPC stage (same fold function, same
symbol order), where the decode's EVM outputs are filled (`evm_fill`, the unchanged block moved into a function). A
decode that returns before the LDPC stage drops its pending sums at its next grant (context released). CHECK grants
never defer. EVMGRANT EVM medians unchanged (N1 60.0, E1 60.1, E2 60.8 %).

## 4. What remains to reach < 0.5 ms (mean)

1. **Protect SMs for the real-time PDSCH kernels** (largest lever: the tails are SM starvation). Options, in order of
   expected payoff: confine CSI-RS discovery to an SM partition (CUDA green contexts, `cudaDevSmResourceSplitByCount`,
   streams created in the partition) or MPS with an SM limit; or cut the discovery kernels into short launches
   (< ~100 us per block) so RT blocks get SMs between them. With that, enable the GPU LDPC (115-125 us per TB for 1-4
   code blocks on a free GPU) and expect the means to approach the p50s (~0.45 ms). Owner: discovery GPU modules +
   orchestration (not changed here).
2. CHECK spot-checks in production: t26q runs DEMOD/CHEST CHECK=64 (a CPU recompute of 1/64 grants, ~1.5 ms each);
   =0 or a larger N for timing-critical OTA runs.
3. Not done (would matter only after 1.): per-slot batching of all consumers' grants in one launch / CUDA graphs (each
   consumer still launches ~12 small kernels per grant, 1-40 us each), FP32 instead of FP64 in ALIGN/MMSE where provably
   exact, the EVM kernels' FP64 reductions, PT-RS on the GPU.
