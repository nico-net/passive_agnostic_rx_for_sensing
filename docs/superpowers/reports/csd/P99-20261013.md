## 1. Verdict

1. **The on-air stale drops are born late in the PDCCH scan pool**, not in the PDSCH queue: r = 0.92 between scan-queue stale %
   and PDSCH stale % over 15 OTA runs; the PDSCH queue wait explains <= 10 % of the stale drops in 12 of 15 runs (section 2.1).
   This is why the OTATAIL GPU-budget fix (a replay-only scan bottleneck) did not carry over to air.
2. **A ~0.95 s whole-decoder freeze mechanism exists and is now explained**: SCHED_FIFO consumers spinning on `cudaStreamQuery`
   starve the bound per-CPU kernel workers (`kworker/N`) of their cores until RT throttling (950 ms of every 1000 ms) lets
   them run; the GPU path waits for that work, so all five consumers freeze together `[MEASURED, DGX air-like replay + perf
   sched]`. New flag **`ISAC_GPU_POLL_SLEEP_US=200`** (poll with a 20 us sleep after 200 us) removes it: real-grant max
   953 -> 47 ms (b, Welch p = 0.001) and 948 -> 111 ms (tim, p = 0.005); real mean 16.1 -> 0.68 ms (b), 8.3 -> 1.4 ms (tim);
   decoded grants +66 % (b, p = 0.02) / +39 % (tim, p = 0.03), CRC-OK +78 % (b, p = 0.09) / +104 % (tim, p = 0.002).
   On air the freeze is the STALLS "class A" 0.86-0.92 s event (once, early); mid-run on-air bodies stay below 0.6 s, so the
   replay over-represents it `[INF]` and the on-air gain will be smaller.
3. **New flags `ISAC_SCANQ_FRESH=6` + `ISAC_PDSCH_RETRY_YIELD=2` (arm PYF, on top of POLL)** remove the born-late stale
   drops in the air-like replay: stale 19.1 +- 17.5 % -> 0.4 +- 0.5 % (b), 4.0 -> 0.7 % (tim, p = 0.07); real jobs born >= 8
   slots old 32 % -> 5 % (b), 9 % -> 1 % (tim); retry bodies per real grant 2.48 -> 1.70 (p = 0.005) with the rescue rate
   unchanged (5.69 % -> 5.16 % of decoded grants on b); real p99 4.1 -> 3.3 ms (b), 10.2 -> 5.7 ms (tim) (n.s., n = 3).
4. **Target p99 < 0.5 ms: NOT MET.** Best replay arm (PYF): real-grant p50 0.40 ms / p90 0.95 ms / **p99 3.3 ms** / mean
   0.62 ms (b); p50 0.47 / p90 1.8 / p99 5.7 ms / mean 1.2 ms (tim). After the freezes and the stale drops, the remaining
   tail is work: large grants (2-4 code blocks per TB on air), LDPC and chest on the 40 real-time SMs shared by 5 consumers,
   1-in-64 CPU references, ~17 launches per body (section 6).
5. CRC: per-bucket CRC-OK counts are not lower on b in any arm; on tim PYF vs POLL the MCS 0-4 count is 968 -> 814 (-16 %),
   MCS 5-9 132 -> 139, MCS 10-16 163 -> 193; total queue CRC-OK 379 -> 342 (Welch p = 0.24, n = 3). Not a shown drop, but
   the reason the OTA plan tests POLL alone first and YIELD/FRESH as a separate arm.
6. Bit-exactness unaffected (no arithmetic touched): `GPU_EXACT_CHEST/DEMOD check_bad = 0`, 0 MISMATCH in all 46 replays.

## 2. Tail decomposition on air (offline read of 15 OTA logs)

### 2.1 Stale drops are born late in the PDCCH scan pool, not in the PDSCH queue

`[MEASURED, OTA 2026-10-12/13, 13 Vodafone runs (t26b M0/M1/M2, K, L) + 2 TIM runs (TmA/TmB)]`. A PDSCH job is stale when the
producer is >= 18 slots (9 ms) past its slot at dequeue (`nr_passive_samples_valid`, `[CODE-READ]`). The PDSCH queue logs its
own wait (`pdschq_latency.enqueue_dequeue`), so the share of stale drops that the PDSCH queue can have caused is bounded by the
share of jobs that waited >= 8 ms:

| run | pdschq stale % | jobs waited >= 8 ms % | scan occasions dropped stale % | scan p50 bucket |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| M0a / M0b / M0c (t26b) | 57.7 / 67.4 / 42.9 | 2.0 / 2.8 / 6.7 | 12.7 / 14.3 / 5.6 | 1049 / 1049 / 524 us |
| M1a / M1b / M1c | 56.6 / 48.0 / 66.5 | 3.0 / 4.7 / 3.3 | 12.1 / 6.4 / 11.1 | 1049 / 524 / 1049 |
| M2a / M2b / M2c | 47.8 / 46.2 / 45.9 | 4.2 / 4.8 / 3.5 | 6.7 / 6.2 / 5.8 | 1049 |
| K2 / K3 (t26bKn) | 59.9 / 23.6 | 0.0 / 13.4 | 13.9 / 1.2 | 524 |
| L2 / L3 (t26bLn) | 46.9 / 22.6 | 3.5 / 3.9 | 6.5 / 2.9 | 1049 / 524 |
| TmA / TmB (TIM, t26z) | 26.7 / 29.2 | 0.4 / 2.6 | 4.7 / 3.5 | -- |

- Pearson r(scan-queue stale %, PDSCH stale %) = **0.92** over the 15 runs; r(PDSCH queue wait >= 8 ms, PDSCH stale %) = -0.39.
- In 12 of 15 runs the PDSCH queue wait explains at most 10 % of the stale drops (K3: at most 57 %, L3 17 %, M0c 16 %).
- So 90+ % of the on-air stale drops are jobs that were already old when the scan pool enqueued them: the scan pool serves
  one occasion per slot (2000 per second, `scanq_queued` +20000 per 10 s in every run) with 2 consumers whose service p50 is
  0.5-1 ms (SCANTIME), i.e. an offered load of ~0.5-1.0 per consumer, and a FIFO queue in front of it. This is the STALLS-20261012
  diagnosis, now with the full 13-run OTA set behind it.
- Why OTATAIL's replay gain (stale 24 -> 3 %) did not carry over `[INF from the configs]`: `timacq_replay.sh` runs without the
  UL-UCI monitor (`pdcch_blind_monitor_ul_uci`, 170 DCI RNTIs and a bank pass per RNTI on air), every thread unpinned at
  SCHED_OTHER/nice 19 over 12 cores (no SYS_NICE in replays: `threadCreate ... no affinity, default priority`), while on air the
  scan pool is two FIFO-50 threads pinned to cores 6 and 7 (core 7 shared with PDSCH consumer 0, core 6 with the PUSCH
  consumer). The replay stale drops (27-32 % in OTATAIL arm A) came from a different scan bottleneck (little cores, SCANTIME p99
  16.8 ms), which the GPU budget relieved; on air the scan pool is CPU-bound.

- On Vodafone 55-66 % of all PDSCH stage time goes to recovery bodies; 95 % of the rescues come from one arm (arm 2, the S=0/1
  start-symbol toggle: K3 4646 of 4873, L2 2865 of 2876); the other arms rescue ~1 % of their tries. On TIM the chains rescue
  **0.1 %** (19 of 21160): the recovery bodies are pure load there. A failing body runs the LDPC to its iteration cap, so these
  are also the most expensive GPU bodies.
- The p99 of a *real* body is 3.4-7.6 ms on air vs 2.3-3.0 ms in the quiet replay (DECODETIME). On air the TBs are bigger
  (2-4 code blocks per TB vs 1.4-1.9 in the replays), LDPC is the largest stage at p99 (2-4 ms), and the 5 consumers run
  retry bodies concurrently with the real ones on the 40 real-time SMs `[INF: contention; nsys on air not available]`.
- `[CODE-READ]` Three bit-exactness self-checks run a full CPU reference inside the decode body, each every 64th call on its own
  counter (`ISAC_PDSCH_GPU_EXACT_CHEST_CHECK=64`, `..._DEMOD_CHECK=64`, `ISAC_CHEST_GPU_DELAY_CHECK` default 64): ~4.7 % of the
  bodies carry a CPU reference, which is more than the 1 % that defines the p99. On replay b the CHECK=0 arm did not move p99
  (DECODETIME §5, small grants); on air the CPU reference of a 3-4 CB / multi-layer grant costs several ms `[INF]`.
- `[CODE-READ]` DECODETIME's lever "LDPC code blocks of one TB in one launch" already exists: `nr_pdsch_gpu_ldpc_run()` decodes up
  to 32 code blocks per launch (one 8-block cluster each).

## 6. What p99 < 0.5 ms needs (design, ordered by the measured contribution)

Measured contributions, in the order to remove them (numbers from sections 2 and 5):

1. **Freezes (done, `ISAC_GPU_POLL_SLEEP_US`)**. Next: give core 6 one RT thread only (STALLS S1: `stl_scan3.cfg` with
   `ISAC_PDSCH_CORES=9,3,4,11,14 ISAC_PUSCH_CORES=2`), and never spin at FIFO on a core whose kworkers the GPU driver needs:
   replace the polls by `cudaEventSynchronize` on a blocking-sync event for waits expected > 200 us (CUDA graphs below make the
   expected time known).
2. **Born-late jobs (done in replay, `ISAC_SCANQ_FRESH`)**. The scan pool runs at an offered load of ~0.5-1.0 per consumer (one
   occasion per slot, 0.5-1 ms service); FRESH only reorders. Capacity: 3 big-core scan consumers (S1) and a GPU bank pass
   (the per-RNTI bank runs ~290 us x 1.75 per occasion on air, `SCANPASS`); then FRESH becomes a safety net.
3. **Recovery bodies (done, `ISAC_PDSCH_RETRY_YIELD=2`)**: 55-66 % of on-air PDSCH stage time; the gate keeps the one arm that
   rescues (S toggle, 28 % on Vodafone) and drops the ~1 %-arms to 1 in 16. Next: learn the S=0/1 alias per RNTI (one body
   instead of two for 95 % of the rescues: `ISAC_PDSCH_RETRY_PREDICT` exists, never measured on air).
4. **CPU references in the body**: three independent 1-in-64 self-checks = ~4.7 % of the bodies carry a CPU reference, above
   the 1 % that sets p99. Move the reference off the decode thread (copy inputs, verify on a SCHED_OTHER worker; disable the GPU
   path on a mismatch as today) or 1 in 1024 per check; the replay arm for it was contaminated (section 5).
5. **Kernel floor (not done; DECODETIME §5 nsys: p99 body = 1.2 ms of kernels + 0.11 ms launch API, ~17 launches)**.
   (a) One CUDA graph per (stage, grant shape) captured at first use of the shape, replayed per grant (shapes are few: Nl x
   n_symb x RB bucket); (b) fuse chest LS+FIR+delay and demod into one persistent kernel per slot group, so all grants of a slot
   (already taken by one consumer, `SLOT GROUP`) go in one launch sequence; (c) LDPC: already one launch per TB (<= 32 CBs,
   `nr_pdsch_gpu_ldpc_run`) -- the p99 comes from failing TBs at the iteration cap and from 8-block clusters x CBs x 5
   consumers on 40 SMs: cap iterations for retry bodies (a failing retry wastes the full cap) and admit at most N concurrent
   LDPC clusters; (d) FP32 chest/demod in a non-CHECK fast mode, with the FP64 bit-exact path kept for CHECK; the demod
   numerics belong to the high-MCS agent and were not touched.
6. **Priority**: real grants of solved RNTIs before probes. On TIM, probe bodies hold 3-17x the stage time of the real
   bodies (section 5, `stage_s`): probes on the low-priority GPU streams (`ISAC_PDSCH_GPU_PRIO=1` exists) and a probe budget
   tied to the queue backlog, like the retry yield.

Expected after 1-3 on air `[INF]`: stale < 10 %, real p50 ~0.4 ms, p90 ~1 ms, p99 3-6 ms. p99 < 0.5 ms needs 4 and 5.
