## Changes and decisions

[CODE-READ] Locations below are relative to `openair1/PHY/NR_UE_TRANSPORT/`:

- `nr_pdcch_blind_monitor.c:994`: separate stage-1/2 queue, enabled only by exact `ISAC_CSIRS_DISC=1`. The previous feature-off handoff is retained. The additional queue does not depend on the walk's found/verified/extent state and never substitutes its geometry into the live root configuration.
- `nr_pdcch_blind_monitor.c:1023`: validate up to 128 lines independently of the eight-entry bank. This matters for the supplied 11-line handoff: the old eight-line reader omitted nID 48960. Use `/tmp/coresets_discovered.txt`, optionally overridden by `ISAC_PDCCH_DISCOVERED_FILE`. Refresh after consuming the current queue, so repeated stage-2 publications cannot restart its first unsuccessful candidate forever. Check actual carrier bounds when starting a dwell.
- `nr_pdcch_blind_monitor.c:1071`: immutable per-pass geometry/nID snapshots and generation tokens. Dwell rejection after 4000 distinct attempted occasions; stale, repeated-slot or unchanged-payload evidence cannot verify. Two accepted DL DCIs with the same RNTI and length, different payloads and increasing slots admit an entry. The RT caller supplies evidence only after CRC, mismatch and existing persistence/confirmation gates. Queue state resets with discovery/identity epochs; already-banked footprints are skipped, and a full bank waits for existing expiry.
- `nr_pdcch_coreset_bank.c:236`, `:372`: identity includes absolute RB position, span, duration, symbol, mapping and nID. Preserve the legacy geometry query; add the symbol-aware query. Discovered entries bypass the walk's same-owner alias exclusion and do not treat a new payload length as a new CORESET. Existing bank revocation, re-verification and expiry remain in use (`ISAC_RECONF=1` enables the existing runtime lifecycle).
- `nr_pdcch_blind_monitor_rt.c:3222`: bank entries, the independent dwell, CORESET#0 USS and the live walk continue within the occasion dispatcher. New dwell evidence is serialized by the existing Phase-2 lock, with immutable snapshots during decoding. The dwell cannot emit PDSCH/CFR before verification. Duplicate operational grants still refresh a discovered bank entry's health.
- `nr_pdcch_blind_monitor_rt.c:449`, `:2749`, `:3660`, `:4462`, `:5117`: use each entry's nID for DM-RS and data descrambling. Discovered identities retain their own confirmed-RNTI exploration even if the live walk has chosen zero globally. Exact-owner DL/UL tasks and length probes use that owner's C-RNTI on non-PCI nIDs at every candidate position. Reject a non-PCI configured-ID decode whose recovered CRC RNTI disagrees with the data-scrambling hypothesis. PCI-equal discovered IDs retain zero/configured-RNTI alternatives because the configured-ID presence bit is unknown.

[CODE-READ] This follows the USS configured-ID rule in [TS 38.211 section 7.3.2.3](https://www.etsi.org/deliver/etsi_ts/138200_138299/138211/18.07.00_60/ts_138211v180700p.pdf): data initialization uses `(C-RNTI * 65536 + configured nID) mod 2^31`. Discovery does not introduce an exhaustive RNTI search; it retains receiver-derived RNTI hypotheses and the existing opt-in joint solver. An unknown UE with no usable RNTI evidence can therefore time out even with correct geometry/nID.

[CODE-READ] Budget: **at most 64 CCE/AL candidates per occasion shared across the bank and one active additional dwell**. Integer remainder rotates; three contexts receive caps 22/21/21, eight receive 8 each. Candidate positions vary with slot and nID, and aggregation-level probe floors shrink with the allocation. Unused shares are not redistributed. This is a candidate cap, not a 64-polar-decode cap: payload-length/format/RNTI hypotheses can add work on those candidates. The existing optional `ISAC_PDCCH_DISCOVERY_BUDGET_US` deadline also applies to additional dwell length work (default unset/zero). CSS0 and the live walk retain their separate existing budgets.

[CODE-READ] Logs include `SENSING: discovered CORESET k/n nid=... dwell start`, `... VERIFIED -> bank add index=... rnti=... len=...`, and `... rejected (reason)`. Invalid geometry and carrier bounds are rejected explicitly. Scores are never sufficient for banking.

[CODE-READ] No edits to `radio/USRP/*`, `nr_csirs_disc*`, `nr_td_*`, `nrLDPC_cuda/*`, `nr_pdsch_gpu*`, frozen captures/configurations, or the `nr_csirs_disc.h` contract. Existing contract semantics are unchanged.
