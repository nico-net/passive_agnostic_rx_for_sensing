# RANK2-WEAK (2026-10-23): weak-layer loss in 2-layer PDSCH, LLR normalisation

Branch `csd/rank2-weak` (off adaptive-rx-UL-DL e9c874c308). Replay only (raw_iq, `build_t26`, FROZEN_20261018.env + sd_t26ul.cfg, 4 RX, SPEED 0.25). Labels: [MEASURED, DGX replay <capture>], [CODE-READ], [INFERRED]. Numbers per capture, never pooled.

## 1. Mechanism found

The loss is not in `log2_maxh`, `w` or G rounding. It is in the K38 LLR normalisation shift `nr_llr_norm_shift` (nr_llr_norm.h) applied before the int8 LDPC decoder, on both CPU (nr_pdsch_passive_decode.c) and GPU (`k_norm_sum`, nr_gpu_pass_graph.cu).
- [CODE-READ] The shift is taken from the mean |LLR| of every 16th LLR. With 2 layers the LLR buffer alternates one modulation symbol (Qm bits) per layer, so for Qm 2, 4 and 8 the stride-16 sample reads layer 0 ONLY, and for Qm 6 it is dominated by the strong layer. The shift is sized by the strong layer.
- [MEASURED, DGX replay raw_pci415_12s_b, CPU path, NR_TEST_RANK2W_DIAG] Pre-shift mean |LLR| layer 0 / layer 1 for gap >= 8 dB: QPSK 201 / 19 (n 27), 16QAM 2442 / 300 (n 6). After the shift (k about 2.6 QPSK, 7 16QAM) the weak layer has mean 2.3 LSB at 16QAM and exact-zero share 22 % (QPSK) to 35 % (16QAM), vs 3 % in the strong layer. Balanced layers (gap < 2): equal means, 3 % zeros in both. The zero share grows monotonically with the gap (QPSK 1.6, 2.7, 8.0, 21.8 % for gap <2, 2-5, 5-8, >=8). This is the int8 quantisation starving the weak layer, matching the OTA gap -> CRC collapse of RANK2-BIG cause 3.
- Ruled out [MEASURED, DGX replay]: MMSE2DIAG-style `w == 0` or singular shares are < 1 %; sat share of the strong layer is < 1 %; the LLRZERO report already showed w is large enough once `L2MAXH_DELTA_NL2 = -3` is applied.

## 2. Fix (one rule, no flag)

`nr_llr_norm_shift_nl(llr, span, nl, qm)` (nr_llr_norm.h): for nl == 2 and Qm 4, 6, 8 sample every 16th RE block (Qm LLRs per layer), k from the mean of both layers, minus e = min(2, floor(log2(strong/weak mean))) bits. Every other case (rank 1, nl >= 3, QPSK) calls the old function: bit for bit. GPU twin `k_norm_sum` identical (PassBlk nl/qm). Call sites: passive decode, GrantWork CB0 input. Test seams (NR_TEST_ prefix): `NR_TEST_L2NORM_OLD=1` (old rule), `NR_TEST_L2NORM_DK`, `NR_TEST_L2NORM_TARGET`, `NR_TEST_RANK2W_DIAG=1`, `NR_TEST_FORCE_CPU_TB=1`.

Design iterations, replay-driven [MEASURED, DGX replay raw_pci415_12s_b, one run each, rank-2 grants paired by (rnti, frame.slot), fail->ok gains vs ok->fail losses; control = old vs old: 8 gains / 7 losses]:
- weak-layer-only sizing (all Qm incl. QPSK): 3-4 gains / 31-44 losses, concentrated in QPSK MCS 0 (35 of 1192). Rejected: QPSK needs no help (CRC already ~100 % at gap >= 8 on air) and saturating the strong layer hurts it.
- same with a cap of 1-3 bits over the strong layer: still 17-47 losses (mostly QPSK).
- final (Qm >= 4 only, mean of both layers, extra bits by ratio capped at 2): rank-2 all 5 gains / 9 losses (old2 vs new), 9 / 19 (old1 vs new2), big grants 1 / 1 and 0 / 1; i.e. at the control level.
