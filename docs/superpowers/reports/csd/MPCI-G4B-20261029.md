# MPCI-G4B (2026-10-29): discovery ticket loose ends

Branch `mpci/c-g4b` (from mpci/int-1029c 0947bfaeb3). Host DGX GB10 aarch64, shared. Labels: [CODE-READ], [OFFLINE VERIFIED, DGX GB10 aarch64 unit]. No replay, radio or bench was run (shared host): the effect on the 2/3 figure is NOT measured here, the OTA lines below are the check.
NO FLAGS: no env var, CLI option or -D added.

## 1. Why cell 0 had about 2/3 of its solo discovery counters with two cells [CODE-READ of nr_gpu_budget.c / nr_gpu_chunk.h / nr_csirs_disc_gpu*.cu, and the G4 DISC_TICKET lines: wait_max 200 ms, held_max 73/29 ms, rt_busy 14-22 %, pace 16-23 s of 240 s on c0]
Three causes, all in the G4 ticket; the order rule is not one of them (same-state cells already alternate by least recent use, 1 s half life; prefer only lifts an ACQUIRE cell above a MAINTAIN cell).
1. **Pace charged while the device was idle.** After each bulk hold h the next bulk grant waited h*(100-cap)/cap, with cap = max(min(100,k*30), 100 - RT-busy share). RT-busy is the wall share with an RT user in flight (14-22 % in the replay), so the cap was 78-86 %, i.e. 14-22 % of every bulk hold was added as forced idle, in every window, also while no RT job was in flight at all (pace_ms 16-23 s on c0 of 240 s). The k*30 term was not the culprit (it is a floor under the cap, `max`, it can only raise it), but the "100 - share" form still throttled a device that was idle at the moment of the grant. Also the share was truncated (a residual 0.3 % gave cap 99, pace 1 %: the smoothed share never reaches 0).
2. **Ticket held across host-side work.** `nr_gpu_chunked` called the RT admission wait (`admit()`, up to 100 us per 1 ms chunk of yield-polling) AFTER taking the bulk ticket: device idle, ticket held, the other cell waiting. The legacy footprint / normalise / accumulate / lattice / fold entries took the ticket before their cudaMalloc / buffer growth (host work, may sync).
3. The serialisation itself (two cells need twice the device time of one) remains: it is the physical limit, not removable.

## Fix
- `nr_gpu_budget.c`: the pace holds a bulk waiter only while an RT user is in flight (checked at release for the stored pace and at the grant, re-polled every 1 ms); an idle device is never throttled. The cap is rounded (a residual share below 0.5 % is cap 100). The cap is still max(k*30 %, idle share): the floor only raises it, so a low RT load gives cap = idle, never the floor; RT busy: unchanged (floor 60 %).
- `nr_gpu_chunk.h`: `admit()` runs before the ticket; `NrGpuDiscTicket(bulk, defer)` + `take()`.
- `nr_csirs_disc_gpu.cu`: footprints / normalize_host / fold_gpu / accumulate_host / lattice_host take the ticket after their allocations (device work only under the ticket).
- Fair order between cells in the same state: unchanged rule, now covered by a test.

## 2. Cell GPU workspace frees
A12 lifecycle [CODE-READ executables/nr-uesoftmodem.c, ru_park.h]: retire = pause (entry paused, ring parked, ru_cell_quiesce, slot threads kept; `mpci_cell_retire` never calls the discovery stop); the final retire/stop is `nr_csirs_disc_cell_release(k)` (N>1) / `nr_csirs_disc_stop()` (N=1) in the shutdown path, which calls `nr_csirs_disc_stop`, after the cell's discovery worker joined. That is where the frees are hooked (`nr_csirs_disc_feed.c`), never on pause.
- New `nr_csirs_disc_gpu_cell_free()` (backend.c, independent of the disable switch and breaker; no-op when the modules were never loaded) calls `nr_csirs_disc_gpu_sweep_cell_free` (sweep workspace: streams, event, all device buffers, host vectors, evidence bank) and `nr_csirs_disc_gpu_cell_free` (stream, event, cuFFT plans, FFT/FEP/accumulate/lattice buffers, resident detector history). Both take the cell's mutexes first, then the discovery ticket (same order as every entry point), drain the stream; idempotent; a later prepare of the cell index recreates everything.
- Ledger returns to 0 for the cell. `tools/mpci/mpci_statics.tsv`: `g_modules_loaded` PROCESS.
