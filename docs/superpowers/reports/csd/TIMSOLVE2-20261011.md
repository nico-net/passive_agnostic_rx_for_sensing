## 1. Task 1: classification of every TIM RNTI without a CRC-OK decode

Unit: an RNTI with >= 1 DCI 1_1 (`rnti_seen fmt=1_1`, SI excluded) and no `EVMGRANT crc=1 probe=0`. Rules, applied in this order
(**[INFERRED]** from **[MEASURED, DGX OTA 2026-10-11, t26t]** log fields):

- **e late first solve**: its last DCI came before (or < 1 s after) the cell's first SOLVED. Nothing could seed it.
- **f solved, no full CRC**: SOLVED, but no full TB ever passed.
- **d1 traffic**: < 10 DCIs while the cell had a solution (too little traffic to search, on any cell).
- **b no RNTI-specific id**: the DM-RS scan never produced an identity that is neither the PCI, nor the CORESET id, nor another
  CRC-OK RNTI's identity, nor voted for >= 3 RNTIs (cell-wide).
- **d2 few right probes**: it had a specific identity, but fewer credited "right-looking" probes on it than the 90th percentile
  that the CRC-OK RNTIs of the same run needed (48 / 77 / 84). Right-looking = (DM-RS = that id, data id = 923, add_pos 1,
  MCS table 1, S 1): every one of the 69 TIM solutions of the three runs uses exactly these dims.
- **a/c exhausted**: more right-looking probes than that percentile, still 0 passes.

**Answer: how much of the TIM gap is SNR and how much is the receiver.** [INFERRED, ranges, n = 3 runs] Of the 155 uncovered
TIM RNTIs, ~20 % (31) had too little traffic to be decoded on any cell, ~25-30 % (the ~38 low-tail RNTIs plus the 6 solved
without a full CRC, plus part of b) are consistent with an SNR limit at this site, and ~50-55 % are receiver-side: the late
first solve (42), probes spent on hypotheses the cell had already ruled out (~30), and no specific DM-RS id (12, partly SNR).
The SNR share is an upper bound for "SNR": the MCS-0 / two-layer decode defect above makes RNTIs look weaker than they are. By
traffic, the receiver already decodes the RNTIs that carry 60-83 % of the DCIs.

## 2. Task 2: fixes (all default off, no cell constants)

| flag | what | why, general |
|---|---|---|
| `ISAC_PDSCH_SOLVER_SIGNIF=1` | An RNTI solves only on significant evidence. Every credited search probe is counted as CRC16 (TBS <= 3824 from the reading's PRBs, S, add_pos, MCS, layers; DM-RS overhead over-counted, so unsure = CRC16) or CRC24. Under "all hypotheses wrong", passes are Poisson with mean n16 2^-16 + n24 2^-24 (Bonferroni over every hypothesis the RNTI tried). The candidate's consistent passes on distinct DCIs solve when P(chance) < 1e-3; CRC16-only evidence needs >= 2 passes. One CRC24 pass can solve at once. Otherwise the candidate keeps collecting passes (`candidate pass ... not yet significant` log). | The false-solve bar scales with the number of probes: a quiet RNTI still solves on 2 passes, a busy one (thousands of probes, as 0x47f0 in ab_Tr2_1_094729) needs 3-4. The alpha is a per-RNTI false-solve rate, not a fitted value. |
| `ISAC_PDSCH_SOLVER_PRIOR_IDS=1` | Once a solution is CRC-proven (>= 1 full TB), an unsolved RNTI's grant first tries the proven layout dims (reading of up to 3 proven reps, their S map, add_pos, table) with EACH of its own blind identities in the proven identity form (TIM: DM-RS = own id, data = 923), rotating which identity goes first. Replaces the one-identity-per-grant prior. | Reuses everything but the identity across RNTIs (one RRC template per cell), targets class d2: after the first solve 73-78 % of the uncovered RNTIs' probes went to forms/dims no solved RNTI uses. On a cell where all UEs use the PCI, there is no own-id hypothesis in the proven form and the old path runs. |
| `ISAC_PDSCH_SOLVER_DMRS_PRIO=1` | Under probe contention (bucket below its burst or ring above its fill), a grant on which no reading's PRBs carry one of the RNTI's own DM-RS identities (same-k' coherence >= 0.5, the existing reading filter) is probed only 1 time in 8 (WHY reason `weak`). | The probes go to grants where the link and the identity are visible; work-conserving (no effect without contention). Targets the bootstrap (class e) and wasted probes on undetectable grants. |

Not done: a CRC24-sized grant preference beyond SIGNIF (the search keeps preferring QPSK one-layer readings, which pass more often
at TIM SNRs); a cell-wide (cross-RNTI) coverage of the non-identity dims before the first solve; the MCS-0 / Nl-2 decode defect.

## 5. Risks and open points

- **No OTA validation.** The replays have ~10 TIM RNTIs and no 30-s bootstrap; the classes that dominate OTA (late first solve,
  probes on ruled-out forms) are only partly present. Gains are [MEASURED] on t12 / t4 / p421 at 1x and neutral elsewhere; vb 1x
  okR 30 -> 25 (n = 1 comparable run each) is a possible small loss.
- **SIGNIF** solves on one CRC24 pass (10 of 158 replay solves). A wrong one-pass solution decodes no full TB and is reopened after
  256 grants; with PRIOR_CRC=1 it never seeds the cell. The CRC24 classification over-counts DM-RS overhead (unsure = CRC16).
- **PRIOR_IDS** puts every own identity on the proven dims before the search; an RNTI whose dims differ from the cell template is
  searched with the remaining budget only (slower, not blocked).
- **DMRS_PRIO** depends on the DM-RS reading filter (coherence >= 0.5); an RNTI whose own identities are wrong, or whose DM-RS is
  below that, is probed on 1 grant in 8 under contention. It runs the filter also for single-reading grants (one cached FEP of
  one symbol per slot on the scan consumer).
- **Not fixed, measured**: TIM MCS 0 grants at EVM SNR >= 10 dB pass CRC only ~50 % (MCS 1-4: 96-100 %), and two-layer grants at
  MCS >= 5 mostly fail; cause not established. This is the largest receiver-side lever left for TIM decode counts.
- Classification limits: the "right identity" of an unsolved RNTI is inferred (scan specificity), not known; SNR of unsolved RNTIs
  is not in the OTA logs (no probe EVM), so the SNR share is a range from the solved population, not a per-RNTI measurement.
