# New cells 508 / 403 / 675 (and PCI 4): receiver sync + MIB, offline replay, 2026-10-11

Evidence labels: [MEASURED, replay] = nr-uesoftmodem build_t26 (replay of 1 s captures `ota_runs/survey_1s_1011`, tiled, DGX); [INFERRED] = reasoning/code reading.
Offline only; no radio. Single site/time/antenna RX1. Receiver args = `timacq_replay.sh` clone (ENVFILE ota/t26t.env, `ISAC_SCAN_ANT=1`).
Scripts: `tests/passive_rx/survey/newcell_replay.sh` (one run), `timacq_nant.sh` (timacq_replay with NANT=1/2/4 RX), `scale_tile.py` (tile + digital gain).

## Root cause of "synch Failed" [MEASURED, replay]
1. NOT k_SSB. The receiver finds the PSS (debug log: `Sync source nid2=1, peak 78 dB over avg 68 dB`) and the SSS gives the right PCI, but the SSS metric
   (11-13k) is below the hard-coded `SSS_METRIC_FLOOR_NR = 30000` (openair1/PHY/NR_REFSIG/sss_nr.h:44, absolute, no env override) -> "Failed to detect SSS after
   PSS, metric of SSS 12484, threshold 30000". The SSB is simply weaker in ADC counts than TIM 923 (PSS peak/avg 10 dB vs 20 dB; capture RMS about 22 counts).
   --ssb / -C choices move the grid only by multiples of 30 kHz and cannot change this (the receiver extracts the PBCH at the same RF position); k_SSB is
   read from the MIB after the PBCH, it is not an input of PSS/SSS. A sweep over --ssb was therefore not run [INFERRED].
2. Fix without rebuilding: digital gain on the saved IQ (`scale_tile.py`, equals a higher RX gain, no clipping except 39 720 samples of 1.9e9 in c3610):
   x4 (508 cell) and x8 (c3610) pass the floor, x32 needed for 675 (SSS metric 11k at x8). Metric scales about linearly with gain.
3. Residual CFO: `--initial-fo -4800` (TIM value) leaves a PSS ffo estimate of +2.8 kHz at 3570 MHz; `-2400` works (measured CFO after sync -620 Hz) for all
   three captures. cfo_est.py (+-7 kHz unambiguous) is aliased for 403 (+2123 = -4917 + 7040).
4. Tiling: 1 s tiled x4 (x10 at gain 8, x6 at gain 32) with `tile.sh` semantics; 1 s = 50 SSB periods so SSB timing is continuous, SFN jumps at each tile boundary
   (visible as different sfn in repeated MIBs). No resync problem seen.

## Antenna dimension (coordinator hint) [MEASURED, replay + INFERRED]
- 4 RX never crashed: 13 runs, all rc=0, no SIGSEGV/assert/abort in run.log (the TLS fix 84c702792f is in this binary). No gdb run was needed.
- Sync was identical for 1, 2 and 4 RX on 403 and PCI 4 (same MIB, PDCCH confirmed). Reason: `ISAC_SCAN_ANT=1` in the env -> `scan_fp.nb_antennas_rx = 1`, the
  scan (PSS/SSS/PBCH) runs on ONE branch chosen by energy (`Scan branch by energy: antenna 0 ... order [0 2 1 3]`); antenna count only affects later stages.
  PSS correlation itself (pss_nr.c) sums |corr|^2 over the antennas it is given (non-coherent, no phase issue); with SCAN_ANT=1 that is a single antenna.
- The failures were SSS_METRIC_FLOOR_NR (amplitude), not the antenna count.

## Recommendation
For live OTA: raise the effective RX gain (X410 gain 38 is what the capture used; digital x4..x8 was needed) or lower the floor via a receiver change
(`SSS_METRIC_FLOOR_NR` scaled by the measured noise / env override); use `--initial-fo` about -2400 at 3.4-3.6 GHz on this X410 today (ppm drifts: -4800 on 10 Oct).
Replay dirs: `replays/replay_nc_*` (ignored/local).
