## 2. What the receiver logs today (a)

Scope codes: **CELL-T** per cell, carried by the `[c<k>]` tag (emitter is the cell's own thread; content is that cell's statics, `NR_CELL_STATIC`); **CELL-F** per cell, named in a field of the line; **RING** per shm ring, `cell=` field (see finding F2); **PROC** process-wide content; **PROC/T** process-wide content that is printed by a cell thread and so carries that thread's tag (the tag is NOT the owner of the numbers); **PROC/U** process-wide and untagged.

Counts: total (c0/c1/untagged) for the two 2-cell fixtures P1 and Q1; total only for the N=1 fixtures S1 and TN1 (all untagged). `[OFFLINE VERIFIED, cloud x86, <fixture> excerpt]`.

### 2.5 Findings that matter for the dashboard

- **F1. The tag of CORE_POOL is the emitter, not the owner.** In Q1 the consecutive `[c0] ... CORE_POOL n=8 yields=32402 ... scan[n=1906664 ...] ... cell0[n=1429513 waited=556535 hold_ms=1128670 ...] cell1[n=1769679 waited=713610 hold_ms=725845 ...]` and `[c1] ... Adding the c0 and c1 copies double-counts; only the `cell<k>[...]` sub-block is per cell, and only that block should be routed by `k`. `hold_ms` is cumulative token-time over all classes (cell0 1128670 ms vs cell1 725845 ms at that line in Q1: a share of 60.9 % / 39.1 % of cumulative hold, an `[ANALYTICAL]` quotient of two printed numbers).
- **F2. MPCI-A8 section "SHMIQ_* lines ... attributable by ring name" holds for READY only. The two `SHMIQ_END` lines are distinguishable only by order.
- **F3. Plan-time lines are untagged and some lack the `SENSING:` prefix** (MPCI_ADMIT, MPCI_CELL id=, via `printf` before `g_log`): a parser must match them by grammar, as MPCI-D2 did for the radio lines.
- **F4. GPU counters are process-wide although tagged.** FEP/DEMOD/CHEST/LDPC `calls=` lines and the GPUPASS census come from module statics (no `NR_CELL_STATIC`); the tag says which cell's thread crossed the 10000-call boundary. In Q1 all 9 `GPU_EXACT_FEP` lines are `[c0]` although both cells decode.
- **F5. `GPU_DISCOVERY_SLICE` has no cell and is a >5 ms event filter**, so it cannot give a per-cell discovery GPU cost.
