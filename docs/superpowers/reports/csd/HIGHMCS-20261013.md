## 2. Why the Nl=2 MCS 17+ bucket is 0 % at 19-25 dB (Vodafone, n=48) [MEASURED replay tim + OTA]
`snr_l=` (new, §6) on the TIM replay: 2-layer grants at `snr` >= 10 have a mean |layer gap| 2.4 dB, `snr` - weak layer 0.9 dB (n=102).
With the ~4-6 dB DD optimism measured for 2-layer QPSK at low SNR (§4) and MCS 17+ needing 15-19 dB (Shannon+1.5), 19-25 dB mean
`snr` leaves the weak layer at or below its requirement. SNR-limited; no 2-layer-specific defect found beyond LLRZERO (already fixed
by `ISAC_L2MAXH_DELTA_NL2=-3`, in t26b).

## 3. Root cause of the receiver-side loss: the per-RNTI receiver pick decodes ~half of the grants from ONE antenna [MEASURED, OTA]

`ISAC_RX_MRC_PER_RNTI=1` (t26b) lets the solver choose per RNTI between receiver 1 (best single branch by |h|^2) and receiver 3
(4-branch MRC + `ISAC_RX_MRC_ALIGN=2`) by Laplace CRC rate (`comb_pick()`, explore 1 in 8); the configured receiver for unsolved
RNTIs is `ISAC_RX_MRC_MODE=1` = single branch. The CRC rate is dominated by MCS-0 grants, which both receivers pass, so the choice is a
tie broken in favour of receiver 1 (first index) and drifts with run noise: Vodafone comb=1 carried 9-22 % (Nl=1) and TIM 28 % of all
grants, more on `old` (68 %). EVMGRANT `comb=` is the receiver of each grant.

## 5. Layers 3-4 [MEASURED, OTA + replay]
Vodafone 4-layer: 36/2077 pass, every grant at `snr` < 9 dB (SNR-limited with 4 RX). Vodafone 3-layer MCS 0-4 at >= 10 dB: 43/43.
TIM 3-layer MCS 0 (QPSK R 0.12, TBS 1480 = Vodafone's) at `snr` 10-12 dB: 0/154 (comb 1 and 3 alike, both runs) -- with a layer-mean
SNR of 10 dB a QPSK R=0.12 codeword cannot fail unless the equaliser output is wrong. TIM branch levels are 20 dB below Vodafone's
(`nvar_db` 15-19 vs 36-40); the stock rank-3/4 QPSK path is the fixed-point adjugate MMSE whose det(G) ~ (|h|^2 >> shift)^4 (code
comment: "determinant underflows at 3-4 layers"); mode 2 (float ZF) is used for QAM at any rank but gated off for QPSK at rank 3-4
(QAM-FIX: ZF noise enhancement cost 16 -> 9 of 246 grants below 10 dB). Together with the 16-18 % fixed-vs-float sign disagreement at
rank 3 (§4) this is `[HYPOTHESIS, measured support]` the TIM rank-3 cause. Not reproducible in replay (tile3 has ~1 rank-3 grant).
