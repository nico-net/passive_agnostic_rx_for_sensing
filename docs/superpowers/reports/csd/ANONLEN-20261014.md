## 1. Where the DL sweep cost is `[MEASURED, DGX replay tim, R, new ANONLEN census, 12 s]`

`nr_pdcch_autodiscover_length_scorer` calls by sweep kind: **per-RNTI first-length sweep 1.72 M calls, 0 hits** (1.6-1.7 M polar decodes, 7000 feeds x 246),
anonymous (n_known = 0 / wide RNTI range) 123 k calls (355 hits = chance-pass rate 0.3 %), second-length hunt 141 k (36 hits), scout 0.
So on TIM the cost is RNTIs that are known (bootstrap) but whose length is not yet locked on that CORESET context, swept over 34 lengths on every
candidate, mostly empty CCEs. On b the same scorer is nearly idle (second-length hunt only): b's anonymous search runs in the pdcchJanon thread
(JOINT_ANON_ASYNC), which this change does not touch.

## 2. What changed (default off; `ISAC_ANONLEN=1` or `ISAC_SCANCOST=3`; `ISAC_ANONLEN_SCREEN=0|1`, `ISAC_ANONLEN_LEARN=0|1` win over the umbrella)

| # | change | where | safety |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Empty-CCE screen computed ONCE per candidate per feed (not per length): amplitude-consistency score below `nbs_cfar_score_min(z=ISAC_ANONLEN_Z, 2.33)` -> not decoded, still counted as a failed trial. With JOINT_ANON the screen is the existing prescreen (same decision, 1 call instead of 34). **Audit:** 1 feed in `ISAC_ANONLEN_AUDIT_EVERY` (64) decodes screened candidates too and logs `ANONLEN_AUDIT_MISS`. | `nr_pdcch_blind_monitor_rt.c` (scorer wrapper, `sweep_ctx.skip`) | can lose only a weak sweep hit (later length lock), never an accepted-DCI task |
| 2 | Learned lengths: lengths locked (CRC-valid, distinct-payload evidence) for >= `ISAC_ANONLEN_MIN_RNTI` (2) RNTIs of this CORESET bank (found[0..1], excluding the 1_0 size) are swept alone; one lap in `ISAC_ANONLEN_EVERY` (16) is a full 34-length lap. Restricted laps do not advance `rot_phase` / `occasions_fed` (the give-up cap counts full laps only). Any RNTI of the bank in SUSPECT (ISAC_RECONF) switches it off. Not applied to scout / second-length hunts. | `nr_pdcch_dci_length_sweep.{h,c}` (`learned_*`, `lap_count`), call site | unit tests (section 5) |
| 3 | Census counters `ANONLEN calls[..] polar[..] hits[..] skipped[..] feeds[..] rounds[..]` (always on, relaxed atomics) | monitor summary line | diagnostics |

Not done: sharing rate matching between lengths (N, E and the sub-block interleaver change with K, so the polar decode cannot reuse more than the unscramble
that is already computed once per candidate) and cross-pass duplicate-decode avoidance (no duplicates found: passes of one occasion rotate lengths via
`rot_phase`, and the FEP cache already covers the shared front end).

## 7. Risks

- Screened weak candidates cannot contribute sweep hits: a weak-SNR UE's length lock comes later (same trade as the SCAN2 UL screen). Not an accepted-DCI loss.
- A reconfigured length on a cell with locked peers is found at most one full lap (16 feeds x stride) later per 5 distinct payloads; SUSPECT contexts disable the restriction at once.
- A wrong lock (chance) in a bank with >= 2 RNTIs agreeing is unlikely; the 1-in-16 full lap bounds the damage.
- Evidence is replay only, one capture where it matters (tim); b is a no-op by construction (async janon path). The second run batch used `ISAC_SCAN2_SLOWPASS_MS=20`.
