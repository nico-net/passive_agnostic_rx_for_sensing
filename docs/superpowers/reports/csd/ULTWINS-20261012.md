## 1. Flags (all need `ISAC_UL_LAYOUT_LOCK=1`; EDGE/COHERENT run on the ULLOCKSPEED sequential machinery)
- `ISAC_UL_LOCK_EDGE=1`: worker (`lp_tab_edge`, nr_pusch_passive_decode.c) takes, for each probe and each hypothesis range [a, a+L), the DM-RS excess e = mean_{rows,ant}(C_q(t*)/(6 nv) - 1) of the PRB just inside and just outside each end (t* = best delay of the inside range, per sequence, rows = the set maximising true+control: symmetric in the two sequences), and returns ze = ((e_in - e_out)_true - (e_in - e_out)_control) * sqrt(nr nant)/2 per edge. The lock (`sprt_feed_locked`) skips an edge whose outside PRB lies in another decoded UL grant (other RNTI) of the same target slot under THAT hypothesis (ring of 8 slots x 24 grants, filled at DCI time), and adds the rest to the slot evidence X_h. Counters edge=used/excluded in STATS and BWP_LOCK (about 76 % used, 24 % excluded on b).
- `ISAC_UL_LOCK_COHERENT=1`: complex per-PRB prefix sums of c_q(t) e^{j theta_t q} (theta_t = 2 pi 12 tau_t / N; heap 3.5 MB/ctx, TLS unchanged), so the range sum |sum_q ...|^2/(6 L nv) - 1 is the coherent sum at delay tau_t (33-tap grid, 16 samples): single-transmitter phase continuity; max over t, true minus control. Residual delay error limits coherence to about L <= 30 PRB at N = 4096; wider ranges degrade to incoherent.
- `ISAC_UL_LOCK_NO_TRIAL_UNLOCKED=1`: `nr_ul_layout_crc_trials()` returns 0 while the BWP is unlocked.
- With EDGE or COHERENT set the incoherent zn term is left out unless `ISAC_UL_LOCK_SPRT=1` too (so each statistic is tested alone); EDGE+COHERENT add. `ISAC_UL_LOCK_CLIP` (default 50) = symmetric clip of each term (diagnostic).
- BWP_LOCK line gained `pair_llr` (min over the 8 best competitors of d^2/(2(W_d+rho)) + 0.5 ln(rho/(W_d+rho)), the log of the pair's mixture martingale) and edge/coh flags and counters.

## 2. False-lock bound [DERIVED]
Unchanged from ULLOCKSPEED: every added term is a function of (true - control) differences with selections (delay, row set, edge exclusion) that are symmetric in the two sequences or depend only on decoded DCIs, so swapping the two sequences of a slot flips all X_h together and M = sqrt(rho/(W+rho)) exp(S^2/(2(W+rho))) is a supermartingale: P(false lock) <= alpha = 1e-3 (Bonferroni over H, pair guard 1e-2 on top) if NO range carries the PUSCH DM-RS sequence. Same caveat as before, and it is the reason for the negative result: the premise fails on a busy cell (other UEs' undecoded DM-RS lie under most ranges; section 3, capture c).

Findings:
1. In every arm the true hypothesis (216@1 on b, 107@2 on t) ranks first or second by S, but the statistic is dominated by W: strong detections (heavy tails) inflate the true layout's W, and a family of unrelated hypotheses (177..180@x, 133@0) reaches z 5-7 on b and **z 9-11 on c, a capture with zero detectable DM-RS (gold=0)**. Those z exceed the Ville bound for H = 192 (c: S^2 = 3.3e6 against a bound of 8e5), i.e. the null "no DM-RS under this range" is false for them: undecoded UEs' DM-RS lie under those ranges. Only the pairwise guard (leader vs close competitor, 179@26 vs 180@28) prevented a false lock on c. The twin problem is therefore not 1-PRB noise only: the edge statistic is no more immune than the range statistic, because a layout hypothesis of the wrong BWP size maps every grant to a shifted position that is still inside a busy contiguous region, and a step in DM-RS energy also occurs at the edges of other UEs' allocations.
2. Edge exclusion removes 24 % of edge observations but the surviving observations (outside PRB empty or belonging to an undecoded UE) are not discriminating: adjacent contiguous packing leaves almost no informative edge.
3. Coherent combining did not separate twins (216@1 z 5.3 vs 215@0 5.9): the twin range shares L-1 of L PRBs of the same transmitter, so the coherent sum differs only by one PRB's contribution, a 1/L effect.
4. NO_TRIAL_UNLOCKED: trials 0 (vs 87-585 on b, 171 on c) and fed/booked 99.9 % vs 89-95 %. In replay the queue is not overloaded (speed 0.25), so the gain over the 27 % OTA feed rate is untested here. It is independent of the statistics and safe to try on OTA together with the legacy rule. Trade-off: CRC-first locking is off until the BWP locks (CRC ~0 at this site).

## 4. Unit tests [UNIT-TESTED, DGX] `test_nr_pdcch_blind_monitor --gtest_filter=UlLayoutLock*` 8/8
New `EdgeTestLocksTheTrueLayoutAndNeverOnNoise`: synthetic per-PRB DM-RS excess (0.8 sigma per PRB on the true 216@1 allocation) with edge statistics only: 216@1 locks for 6 seeds in <= 6000 probes (never a neighbour); 150 noise-only sessions x 1500 probes with heavy-tailed noise: 0 locks. Synthetic only: it has no undecoded neighbours, which is exactly what breaks the real data.

## 5. OTA env delta
Recommended: none for EDGE/COHERENT (do not set). Optional, safe: `ISAC_UL_LOCK_NO_TRIAL_UNLOCKED=1` (more probes reach the worker while unlocked). Rest of t26z unchanged.

## 6. What would help (not done) [INF]
A statistic that is immune to undecoded neighbours needs information the PUSCH DM-RS cannot give: a time-domain constraint (UEs with the same RNTI keep their PRB count L per HARQ-retransmission, or DCI 0_1 RIV re-use across slots of the same RNTI), the SIB1/RRC UL BWP prior (ISAC_UL_LOCK_SIB1_FIRST already), or a TB CRC. Per-RNTI consistency (the same UE's PRB start moves by whole grant lengths slot to slot under the true BWP, not under twins) is the next candidate.
