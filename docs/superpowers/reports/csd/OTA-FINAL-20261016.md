Tail and CRC differences therefore track traffic mix, not arm.
- Like-for-like class only: broadcast class (0xffff) p99 is 1.1-9.3 ms in every run, CRC 96-100 %, never <0.5 ms. [MEASURED, V low load, n=2, n_bc=53-699 per run]
- Late RECOV rescues are large on V when the C-RNTI traffic exists: Va2 4569 rescued of 6535 submitted, Vs1 1056/2579, Vs2 847/2739, VsP2 1256/2790, VaP2 505/977 (late p50/p99 1/15 to 1/28 ms) which lifts effective CRC (Va2 54.7 -> 86.0 %).
