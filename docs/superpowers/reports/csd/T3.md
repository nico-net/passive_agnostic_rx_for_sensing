## Decisions and limitations

- [CODE-READ] `nr_csirs_disc_fp_t` represents a rectangular symbol-mask × subcarrier-mask footprint. An isolated RE
  loss in a multi-symbol pattern cannot always be represented without removing the corresponding mask column/row;
  near matching therefore applies the specified IoU >= 0.8 to the representable Cartesian footprint.
- [CODE-READ] Exact lookup is hash-indexed. Near matching scans the fixed, lazily built legal table, then maintains a
  bounded ranked output online; it never allocates per call.
- [CODE-READ] The feature remains inert unless its U1/U5 caller enables the discovery pipeline through
  `ISAC_CSIRS_DISC=1`; T3 adds no standalone runtime hook and does not change feature-off behavior.
