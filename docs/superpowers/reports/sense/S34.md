- [CODE-READ, DGX aarch64] Merged `sense/t4-gating` and `sense/t3-records` in that order. The blind monitor's only textual conflict uses `nr_isac_submit_cfr_multi_meta(..., dmrs_source, ..., &rs_meta)`. Thus either `pdsch_dmrs` or `pdsch_dmrs_blind` enables its CFR tap while the schema-2 RS metadata is retained. The reviewed disabled baseline and v1-superset/no-double-submit properties remain the merge basis.
- [CODE-READ, DGX aarch64] The PUSCH decoder stores the actual signed FFT advance selected by the TA sweep and any residual-delay refinement in its result. UL grant, DM-RS RS, and data-aided RS records emit `ta_applied_samples` with a separate validity bit, so negative and zero advances serialize as numbers. `ta_est_samples` is null in live UL records because the receiver does not establish a separate per-grant UE timing estimate. Positive `ta_applied_samples` means the UL FFT window was advanced relative to the DL frame.
- [CODE-READ, DGX aarch64] UL grants capture the configuration stamp on entering `NR_CFG_EPOCH_WORK`, before decode and deferred record emission. The PDSCH queue reads epoch and identity generation from one coherent CFR stamp at admission, while retaining its work epoch for stale-job rejection. The sensing stamp reads are skipped when sensing is disabled.
- [CODE-READ, DGX aarch64] PUSCH DM-RS `ports` is a count, with the original bitmask in `port_mask`. SSB `start_rb` uses the Point-A/CRB offset instead of a possibly wrapped FFT bin. UL `dci_slot` is null because the decoded grant does not carry the true DCI slot; the former `slot-k2` guess was removed. `transform_precoding` is taken from the grant waveform state used by the decoder.
