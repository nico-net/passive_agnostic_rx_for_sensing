## Traffic mode: what we extract
Detail: `TRAFFIC-CAPABILITY-20261105.md`.

**DL.** Every DCI 1_0 and 1_1 on every PCI is recorded at accept, with raw bits.
- Layouts: 1_1 layouts are learned per RNTI. 71-94 % of DL 1_1 records carry a solved layout [OTA LOW LOAD].
- Fields: harq_pid, ndi, rv and mcs are non-null in 95-97 % of them.
- k1/dai/tpc/pri: 13-20 % non-null, from the E1 bit prover; the baseline had 0 %.
- nl, qm, tbs (E7): printed only from proven inputs. tbs needs a CRC-learned k0, a full-DL slot and a confirmed
  xOverhead, so it is still rare.
- E3, CRC-free solve by DM-RS physics: never fired on air (e3_phys_solved = 0). Family accepts happen, but their
  grant fields stay null until verified.
- PRBs: allocations up to 217 PRB; 13-19 % of DL grants are at least 128 PRB.
- MCS: 0-19 at night, table 0.

**UL.** Every DCI 0_0 and 0_1 is recorded.
- Daytime: the UL layout locks and 92-97 % of UL records carry the fields [MEASURED OTA].
- Night: on 415 the UL layout lock needs CRC passes that never come.
- UL data is not decodable from our position: PUSCH CRC-OK is at most 0.007 % in every run. Only the UL DCI fields
  and layouts are extracted, not the payloads. Traffic mode therefore decodes PUSCH only until the UL lock, then 1
  grant in 32 per cell.

## Sensing mode: what we extract

Final head 699340171b (C1/I1-I3 fixed). A/B against legacy, 07:02-08:04 [MEASURED OTA, early morning], campaign
tm_ota_20261105_070225, single 923 and pair 415+923, 3 runs per arm:
- **Runs:** 12/12 ok, no crash.
- **Drops on the pair:** scan drops are lower in sensing (0.7-2.7 % against 3.5-10.5 % for legacy). PDSCH queue
  drops are present in both arms.
- **Wideband CFR:** valid wideband CFR records (>= 128 valid PRB) now come out on air, up to 6419 per run on pair 923
  and 1769 on single 923. Before the fix there were 0.
- **Lower total CRC-OK in sensing is by design, not a defect.** The investigation is in
  .superpowers/sdd/2026-11-03-sensing-mode/sensing-crc-investigation.md. Single 923, legacy against sensing
  [MEASURED OTA]:
  - Wide grants (>= 128 PRB): 733 decodes (199 CRC OK, 27 %) against 4026 (2810 CRC OK, 70 %). That is about 14x
    more wide-grant passes, which is the sensing goal.
  - Narrow solved grants: legacy decodes them all; sensing decodes 1 in 32 unless the unit is enabled. 43.3k decodes
    (38.6k OK) against 4.4k (3.8k OK); the pass rate is unchanged at 86-90 %.
  - The solver solves as many RNTIs as in legacy. Decodes are not starved (stale drops are 0.5 %).
  - Total CRC-OK is therefore the wrong metric for sensing. Wide-grant CRC and exported data-aided CFR are the right
    ones.
- **Violations (1-1501 per run)** come from SRS and PUSCH DM-RS export drops (SRS jobs dropped up to 4.3k per run),
  not from PDSCH decode. This is an open item.
