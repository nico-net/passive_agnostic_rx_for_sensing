Definitions. Wide = nb_rb >= 128. Grant counts come from EVMGRANT lines (run.log, probe=1 excluded, de-duplicated per rnti+frame.slot, best crc/cb_ok kept). TB CRC-ok = crc=1. Partial-TB proven = crc=0, cb_total>1 and cb_ok>=1. "Proven" = CRC-ok + partial-proven. Rates are per second of the rs-record span (~212 s). EVMGRANT<->record match key = (rnti, abs_slot mod 20480) because EVMGRANT prints frame.slot only (10.24 s wrap; a rare false match is possible, not quantified). Latency = EVMGRANT dec_us (decoder time, not end-to-end). Availability = fraction of consecutive windows over the span holding >= 1 record with valid_cfr_prbs >= 128. Values are mean (min-max) over 5 runs. "Outside spread" = every NEW run is above (or below) every BASE run.

CAUTION: offered load swings 2-3x between runs of the same arm (415 wide decodes/s BASE 45-152, NEW 30-168; 923 BASE 12-18, NEW 4-26). With n=5 and unmatched load, raw rates are not separable from load; per-wide-grant rates are given for that reason.

## Binding reading (counters, inference from them)

The change shows up in coverage, not in grant decode: wide decode/CRC rates are inside the BASE spread in both cells (load-dominated). What moved clearly is the record side: every proven wide grant now has a pdsch record (BASE 42-55 %), and the share with >= 128 valid PRB went 35.5 -> 73.2 % (415) and 32.8 -> 86.4 % (923), outside the BASE spread; the estimate source is the decoder for 98.5-99.7 % of pdsch_dmrs records (own FEP+chest 0.26 % / 1.5 %). Costs: pdsch record rate 4.5x (415) / 6x (923), scanq stale up on 415, async DA drop up (up to 17 % of that queue in the busiest run), CPU +0.0-0.2 cores (within spread). Wideband availability rose on PDSCH-only sources in the means, but ranges overlap, so n=5 under a 2-3x load swing does not establish it. Not measured: whether a stale-scan or async-DA drop removes a specific proven wide grant (no per-grant drop attribution).
