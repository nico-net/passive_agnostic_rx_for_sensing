## Binding limit (counters, inference from them)

Wideband gain is small and not separable from the load swing: in 415 pdsch_dmrs wideband rec/s is 4.75 BASE vs 4.42 NEW, coverage 8.3 vs 8.1 %; in 923 it is 1.6 vs 5.2 /s and coverage 12 vs 27 % (best case, 3 runs, ranges overlap; wide-grant rate was also 50 % higher in NEW). Availability is unchanged except 415 at 1 s (0.26 vs 0.43, ranges overlap), and 923 is saturated at 1.0 by CSI-RS (110-135 wideband records/s) so it cannot discriminate.

The wbg path itself delivers 0.66-2.1 taps/s. The funnel for NEW arms (wide DL grants of the run = 100 %):
1. Never usable: 44-70 % of wide grants have CRC fail (415: 56, 65, 52 %; 923: 51, 65, 70 %); wbg is CRC-gated, so this alone caps it. All wide grants are rnti_class 0 (C-RNTI), so no RNTI-class loss.
2. Offered (monitor gate: C-RNTI, proven fields, settled sweep) is only 24-61 % of the CRC-ok wide grants (415: 35, 52, 24 %; 923: 50, 61, 32 %), i.e. 8-23 % of all wide grants. Why the rest is not offered is not logged; solved-layout/proven-field state of the RNTI is the likely cause (not measured).
3. Admitted/offered is 9-41 %. This is the token bucket (rate_hz from the scheduler; it sat at the 0.25 /s startup/floor value at the end of 4 of 6 runs and averaged 1.7-9.8 /s) refilling slower than offers arrive (offered_hz 2-8 /s). Refunds are 0 and taps == admitted, so refunds and tap failures are not the limit.
Binding limit: the token rate (step 3), after the offer funnel (steps 1-2). It is a policy limit, not CPU: a tap costs ~0.35 ms and total wbg CPU is under 1 ms/s.

## Surprises

1. Load varies 2x between same-arm runs, which dominates every A/B delta; three runs per arm are not enough to claim a wideband gain or loss.
2. 923 availability is trivially 1.0 because CSI-RS records are wideband; use pdsch-only availability for the wbg question (not computed here).
3. rate_hz sits at the 0.25 /s floor at the end of most NEW runs while the scheduler binding reported none/gpu/pool/queue_ul variously, so the scheduler's published wbg rate is far below what the CPU cost would allow.
4. CPU is the same in both builds (7.1-7.9 cores).
